# WEEPING WILLOW — CHARACTER IMAGE PROMPTS

Prompts designed for ChatGPT / DALL-E. Visual style inspired by The Veil Protocol — cinematic, atmospheric, dark — adapted for Weeping Willow's coastal bio-horror aesthetic.

**DALL-E safety note:** All characters are fully clothed. Descriptions focus on clothing, expression, environment, and mood. Avoid skin-texture language.

---

## STYLE GUIDE (Apply to all prompts)

**Base style direction:** Cinematic portrait, photorealistic with slight stylization. Dark, atmospheric, muted color grading. Inspired by modern sci-fi thriller book covers and concept art.

**Palette:** Desaturated — cold grays, muted steel blues, pale seafoam greens, washed-out amber. Occasional warm highlight (a single light source, a reflection) against an otherwise cold frame. No bright or saturated colors.

**Lighting:** Low-key cinematic lighting. Single directional light source or diffused overcast. Deep shadows. Rim lighting to separate subjects from background. Fog, haze, or atmospheric particulate in the air.

**Texture:** Slight film grain. Cool color cast. Surfaces feel damp, industrial, weathered.

**Composition:** Tight framing — head and shoulders or three-quarter portraits. Characters slightly off-center. Negative space used deliberately. Backgrounds soft-focused or obscured by atmosphere.

**Mood:** Quiet dread. Stillness. Isolation. Not action — presence.

## THE WEEPING WILLOW

### Prompt — Book 1 (Raw / Instinctive)
```
Cinematic portrait of a tall, unsettling female figure standing in a dim industrial corridor. She wears a long, dark, high-collared coat that hangs to her ankles — the fabric looks almost organic, matte and textured like weathered material. Her proportions are slightly wrong — limbs a fraction too long, posture tilted forward with predatory balance. Her face is almost human but not quite — pale complexion, micro-asymmetries in her features, eyes that track with unnatural precision. Expression neutral, unreadable. Her hands hang at her sides — fingers slightly too long, unnervingly still. Dark veining is faintly visible along her neck and jawline, like shadows beneath the surface. Partial fluorescent light from above, most of the frame in deep shadow. Concrete walls. Fog at floor level. Cold steel-blue and gray palette. Film grain. Photorealistic with slight stylization. Horror sci-fi tone — deeply unsettling through stillness, not violence.
```

### Prompt — Book 3 (Refined / Near-Human)
```
Cinematic portrait of a tall woman standing in soft natural light near a window. She wears a simple, clean, dark turtleneck and fitted coat — understated, almost elegant. She appears nearly human — poised, composed, controlled. But something is subtly wrong. Her proportions are slightly elongated. Her expression is warm but arrives a fraction too late, like emotion performed from memory. Her fingers, resting on a windowsill, are just slightly too long, joints too smooth. Faint dark veining barely visible along her neck above the collar. The space around her is domestic, quiet, almost peaceful. Muted color palette — pale gray, washed amber, soft white. Shallow depth of field. Photorealistic. The unsettling quality is in how normal she looks. Atmospheric, calm, wrong.
```

## NOTES FOR IMAGE GENERATION

- **Consistency:** When generating multiple characters, re-paste the Style Guide section at the top of each prompt session to maintain visual consistency across the set.
- **Variations:** For alternate angles or compositions, add "three-quarter view," "profile shot," or "environmental wide shot" to the end of any prompt.
- **Series branding:** For cover-style images, append: "Book cover composition. Title space at top. Author name space at bottom. Minimal typography-safe layout."
- **Before/After pairs:** Caleb's two prompts are designed to be generated as a matched pair. Same framing, different soul. Consider doing this for Enzo as well (before Taking / after Taking).
- **The Veil Protocol alignment:** These prompts share the same DNA — cinematic, dark, atmospheric, character-driven — but swap neon/digital for fog/organic and surveillance for stillness. Same energy, different frequency.
- **DALL-E tips:** If a prompt still gets flagged, try removing emotional descriptors like "predatory" or "unsettling" and replace with neutral framing language. Keep horror in the environment and composition, not in body descriptions.
