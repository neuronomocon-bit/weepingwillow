# WEEPING WILLOW — CHARACTER IMAGE PROMPTS

Prompts designed for ChatGPT / DALL-E. Visual style inspired by The Veil Protocol — cinematic, atmospheric, dark — adapted for Weeping Willow's coastal bio-horror aesthetic.

---

## STYLE GUIDE (Apply to all prompts)

**Base style direction:** Cinematic portrait, photorealistic with slight stylization. Dark, atmospheric, muted color grading. Inspired by modern sci-fi thriller book covers and concept art.

**Palette:** Desaturated — cold grays, muted steel blues, pale seafoam greens, washed-out amber. Occasional warm highlight (a single light source, a reflection) against an otherwise cold frame. No bright or saturated colors.

**Lighting:** Low-key cinematic lighting. Single directional light source or diffused overcast. Deep shadows. Rim lighting to separate subjects from background. Fog, haze, or atmospheric particulate in the air.

**Texture:** Slight film grain. Cool color cast. Surfaces feel damp, industrial, weathered. Organic materials look subtly wrong.

**Composition:** Tight framing — head and shoulders or three-quarter portraits. Characters slightly off-center. Negative space used deliberately. Backgrounds soft-focused or obscured by atmosphere.

**Mood:** Quiet dread. Stillness. Isolation. Not action — presence.

## THE WEEPING WILLOW

### Prompt — Book 1 (Raw / Instinctive)
```
Cinematic portrait of a tall, elongated female figure standing in a dim industrial corridor. Slightly too-long limbs, subtle forward tilt to her posture. Skin is pale, almost translucent — beneath it, faint dark filaments are barely visible, like veins made of cable. Her face is almost human but not quite — micro-asymmetries, eyes that track with unnatural precision. Expression neutral, unreadable. Along her spine, a raised ridge with embedded tendril-like structures, faintly pulsing. She wears no distinct clothing — her surface alternates between smooth ceramic-like patches and matte organic texture, as if skin and material have no boundary. Partial fluorescent light from above, most of the frame in shadow. Concrete walls. Fog at floor level. Cold steel-blue and gray palette. Film grain. Photorealistic with slight stylization. Horror sci-fi tone — not violent, deeply unsettling through stillness.
```

### Prompt — Book 3 (Refined / Near-Human)
```
Cinematic portrait of a tall woman standing in soft natural light near a window. She appears almost entirely human now — poised, composed, elegant. But something is wrong. Her proportions are slightly elongated. Her expression is warm but arrives a fraction too late, like emotion being performed from memory. Skin smooth, unnaturally even. Hands resting at her sides — fingers just slightly too long, joints articulating too smoothly. She wears simple, clean clothing. The space around her is domestic, quiet, almost peaceful. But the light falls on her skin in a way that hints at something beneath — the faintest shadow of dark filaments under the surface. Muted color palette — pale gray, washed amber, soft white. Shallow depth of field. Photorealistic. The horror is in how normal she looks. Atmospheric, unsettling, calm.
```

## IRIS VALE

### Prompt — Book 1 (Post-Incident, Early)
```
Cinematic portrait of a woman in her early 30s, sharp features, dark hair pulled back loosely. She has an observant, controlled expression — watchful but emotionally flat. Eyes intelligent but distant, like she's monitoring herself from the inside. She wears practical work clothing — a dark jacket, utility layers, nothing decorative. Rim lighting from behind separates her from the dark background. Film grain. Fog in the distance. Photorealistic, cinematic framing. The mood is isolation, competence, and something missing behind the eyes.
```

### Prompt — Book 3 (Degraded, Fragmented)
```
Cinematic portrait of the same woman, visibly worn. Thinner face, darker circles under her eyes. Her expression is not sad — it's reduced. Fewer micro-expressions, less movement in the features. She sits in a small apartment surrounded by handwritten notes pinned to every surface — walls, cabinets, a mirror. The notes are her external memory. She holds one in her hand, reading it with careful attention but no visible emotion. Warm lamplight from one source, the rest of the room in shadow. The apartment is sparse, functional, covered in paper. Palette: muted amber and cold gray. Shallow depth of field — the notes behind her are soft, the one in her hand is sharp. Photorealistic. The mood is quiet collapse — a person holding herself together with systems because the internal architecture is failing.
```

## CALEB WARD

### Prompt — Before Taking
```
Cinematic portrait of a man in his early 30s. Open face, easy expression, a half-grin that suggests he just said something sharp. Dark hair, slightly unkempt. He wears a worn work jacket, sleeves pushed up, hands that have done physical work. He's leaning against a wall in an industrial space — a warehouse or converted storage facility, exposed beams, ambient light filtering through high windows. His posture is relaxed, confident, slightly reckless. There's warmth in him — he's the most alive person in the frame. Palette: warm amber and golden tones cutting through industrial gray. Natural light from above. Film grain. Photorealistic. The mood is grounded humanity — a real person, sharp and present, the kind of warmth you notice most after it's gone.
```

### Prompt — After Taking
```
Cinematic portrait of the same man. Same face, same clothes, same setting. But everything is different. His expression is pleasant, neutral, correct. The half-grin is gone — replaced by something polite. His eyes are open, attentive, and completely empty behind the attention. His posture is still relaxed but the energy behind it is absent. He looks directly at camera with mild, cooperative interest. Nothing is wrong with him. That is exactly what is wrong with him. Palette shifts colder — the warm amber is gone, replaced by flat gray-blue. Same industrial space but the light is even, clinical. Film grain. Photorealistic. The mood is absence — a person who is present and accounted for and no longer there.
```

## NOTES FOR IMAGE GENERATION

- **Consistency:** When generating multiple characters, re-paste the Style Guide section at the top of each prompt session to maintain visual consistency across the set.
- **Variations:** For alternate angles or compositions, add "three-quarter view," "profile shot," or "environmental wide shot" to the end of any prompt.
- **Series branding:** For cover-style images, append: "Book cover composition. Title space at top. Author name space at bottom. Minimal typography-safe layout."
- **Before/After pairs:** Caleb's two prompts are designed to be generated as a matched pair. Same framing, different soul. Consider doing this for Enzo as well (before Taking / after Taking).
- **The Veil Protocol alignment:** These prompts share the same DNA — cinematic, dark, atmospheric, character-driven — but swap neon/digital for fog/organic and surveillance for stillness. Same energy, different frequency.
