# WEEPING WILLOW — CHARACTER IMAGE PROMPTS

Prompts designed for ChatGPT / DALL-E. Visual style: dark gothic portraiture, painterly, near-monochrome. Inspired by The Veil Protocol aesthetic, calibrated against reference images for Willow.

**DALL-E safety note:** All human characters are fully clothed. Willow's forms use organic/biomechanical structure language, not body description. If a prompt gets flagged, remove words like "predatory" or "unsettling" and replace with neutral framing.

---

## STYLE GUIDE (Apply to all prompts)

**Base style direction:** Dark gothic portraiture. Painterly and textural, not clean photorealism. Closer to dark fantasy concept art or horror book cover illustration. Rich in shadow, atmosphere, and surface detail.

**Palette:** Near-monochrome. Deep blacks, charcoal grays, muted sepia and umber. Minimal color — where it appears, it's desaturated and earned: a faint amber from a lamp, a cold steel-blue edge light, a washed-out green. The overall impression should be dark, wet, heavy.

**Lighting:** Dramatic chiaroscuro. Deep directional light carving features out of darkness. Heavy shadow. Rim lighting and edge glow. Light sources feel distant or indirect — the subject is always partially consumed by the dark around them.

**Texture:** Painterly grain and atmosphere. Surfaces feel damp, organic, weathered. Backgrounds dissolve into smoke, fog, or textured darkness. Nothing is clean. The air itself has weight.

**Composition:** Tight portraiture — head and shoulders or three-quarter. Subjects emerge from darkness rather than standing in front of it. Backgrounds are atmosphere, not location. Negative space is shadow.

**Mood:** Gothic dread. Stillness. Weight. Isolation. Beauty and wrongness coexisting. Not action, not gore — presence and absence.

**Reference touchstones:** Dark fantasy concept art, Beksinski, gothic horror book covers, wet-texture portraiture.

## THE WEEPING WILLOW

### Prompt — True Form (Raw / Biomechanical)
```
Dark fantasy portrait of a biomechanical female entity emerging from darkness. Her face is human at the center — pale, angular, intense eyes — but the humanity ends at the edges. From her head and shoulders, dense organic structures radiate outward: tangled dark tendrils, web-like filament networks, cable-like growths that drip and hang like wet roots. The structures are layered — some smooth and chitinous, others fibrous and raw, all of them alive. Her body is encased in this biomechanical growth — it is not clothing, it is her. Ridged, textured, layered organic armor that blends seamlessly from her form into the tendrils. Porous, honeycombed sections visible on the shoulders and torso structure. The spine ridge is prominent — raised, embedded with thicker tendril bundles. Everything is wet, dark, and heavy. Near-monochrome palette — deep blacks, charcoal, dark umber. Single faint light source from above catching the ridges and moisture on the surfaces. Smoke and particulate in the air. Painterly, textural, dark fantasy concept art style. The mood is ancient, patient, and deeply wrong.
```

### Prompt — Human Form (Refined / Passing)
```
Dark gothic portrait of a striking woman with long, dark, wet-looking hair that falls past her shoulders in heavy strands. She is beautiful in a way that feels studied rather than natural — features too precise, symmetry too deliberate, as if assembled from observation rather than born. She wears a dark, fitted garment with organic texture — the fabric appears to merge with her hair at the edges, strands and material becoming indistinguishable. Her expression is direct, intense, controlled — she is looking at the viewer with calm awareness. Her eyes are sharp and knowing. Behind her, the background dissolves into dark, smoky atmosphere with faint vertical streaks — like dripping water or hanging filaments, barely visible in the murk. Near-monochrome palette — deep blacks, dark sepia, muted umber. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting from one side, the other half of her face in deep shadow. Painterly, textural, gothic horror portrait style. She looks human. She is not. The wrongness is in the perfection.
```

## IRIS VALE

### Prompt — Book 1 (Post-Incident, Early)
```
Dark gothic portrait of a woman in her early 30s. Sharp, angular features. Dark hair pulled back loosely, a few strands fallen forward. Her expression is controlled, watchful, emotionally flat — intelligent eyes that seem to be looking inward as much as outward. She wears a dark, layered field jacket — heavy, practical, worn at the seams. The collar is turned up. She stands in near-darkness — behind her, the faint suggestion of an industrial corridor, concrete, a distant fluorescent light casting cold blue. Most of the frame is shadow. She emerges from it rather than standing in front of it. Her face is lit from one side — sharp cheekbones, dark circles forming under her eyes, tension in the jaw. The other side falls into black. Painterly texture. Near-monochrome — charcoal, cold blue-gray, desaturated green. Atmosphere is heavy, damp, industrial. The mood is competence hollowed out — someone still functioning, but with something fundamental missing behind the eyes.
```

### Prompt — Book 3 (Degraded, Fragmented)
```
Dark gothic portrait of the same woman, visibly diminished. Thinner face, sharper angles, deeper shadows under the eyes. She wears an oversized dark sweater, sleeves pulled long over her hands. She sits in a dim space — behind her, barely visible in the murk, the shapes of handwritten notes pinned to a wall, dozens of them, layered and overlapping. She holds one small piece of paper in her hands, reading it with careful attention and no expression. A single warm light source — a lamp, low and amber — catches one side of her face. The rest dissolves into textured darkness. Her eyes are still sharp but reduced — fewer thoughts behind them, less movement in the features. Painterly, atmospheric. Palette: dark amber against deep charcoal and black. The mood is quiet disintegration — a person maintained by paper and routine, the internal architecture crumbling.
```

## CALEB WARD

### Prompt — Before Taking
```
Dark gothic portrait of a man in his early 30s. Open, expressive face — a half-grin, warmth in the eyes, the look of someone mid-conversation. Dark hair, slightly unkempt. He wears a worn canvas jacket over a dark t-shirt. He is the warmest subject in the series — the light finds him more than others. ### Prompt — After Taking
```
Dark gothic portrait of the same man. Same face. Same jacket. Same framing. But the warmth is gone. His expression is pleasant, neutral, polite — a faint smile that reaches nowhere. His eyes are open, attentive, and empty. The half-grin has been replaced by something correct. He looks at the viewer with mild, cooperative interest. The amber light is gone — replaced by flat, cold, even illumination. The shadows around him are the same depth but feel heavier, closer. Same painterly texture, same composition. Palette shifts cold — steel gray, desaturated blue, the warmth drained from the frame entirely. The mood is absence wearing a familiar face. Nothing is wrong with him. That is what is wrong.
```

## WESTON

```
Dark gothic portrait of a man in his late 30s — but unlike every other portrait in this series, the darkness is pulled back. The frame is brighter, flatter, more open. His face carries the bone structure of someone who was once rough and tense — hard brow, deep-set eyes, jaw that used to clench. But now he is calm. His expression is pleasant, unbothered, clear. ## NOTES FOR IMAGE GENERATION

- **Consistency:** Re-paste the Style Guide at the top of each DALL-E session. The gothic/painterly direction needs reinforcement or it will drift toward clean photorealism.
- **Willow reference images:** True form and human form references are saved in `C:\Users\kriss\Dropbox\weepingwillow\` — use these as visual anchors when regenerating or iterating.
- **Warmth hierarchy:** Lena > Enzo > Caleb (before) > Joe E. > everyone else. These are the emotionally rich characters — their portraits should feel warmer than the rest of the set. When they're Taken, the warmth drains.
- **Before/After pairs:** Caleb's two prompts are designed as a matched pair — same composition, different soul. Consider doing the same for Enzo (before/after Taking).
- **Weston is the outlier:** His portrait deliberately breaks the dark aesthetic. The flatness and brightness IS the horror. Don't darken it to match the set — the contrast is the point.
- **The Veil Protocol alignment:** Same DNA — cinematic, atmospheric, character-driven — shifted from digital/neon to organic/gothic. Wet textures instead of scanlines. Tendrils instead of data streams. Same energy, different frequency.
- **DALL-E tips:** If flagged, remove "predatory," "unsettling," or "intense" and replace with neutral descriptors. For Willow's true form, emphasize "dark fantasy creature design" and "biomechanical armor" to frame it as creature/character design rather than figure study.
