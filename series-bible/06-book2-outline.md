# BOOK 2 — RE-PLANNING FROM SCRATCH (2026-08-27)

**There is no current Book 2 outline.** This file holds the constraints and the open decisions until one exists.

Book 2 has been planned three times and abandoned three times. On 2026-08-27 the decision was taken to re-plan it from the arc up rather than patch the third attempt. **`13-arc-revision.md` is now history, not instruction.**

Everything from all three attempts is preserved in `archive/`. Nothing has been deleted.

---

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY FIXED

Only two things constrain Book 2 absolutely. Everything else is open.

### 1. Book 1 — The Absence is published and cannot change

22 chapters, 23,405 words. Its text is law. The load-bearing facts a sequel inherits:

- Iris Vale survived direct contact. **What was taken is her ability to feel love for one specific person, her sister Lena.** She remembers Lena and feels nothing.
- Collateral damage: emotional delay, decision instability, memory gaps under stress, occasional false certainty, performed emotion.
- She can sense the entity's proximity. Residual connection from an interrupted contact.
- **Caleb Ward was Taken on the page** (Ch12, his POV) and came back functional and hollow. He is alive at the end of Book 1.
- Rourke has hundreds of cases classified as behavioral flattening, dissociative presentation, emotional attenuation. **No full recoveries.** Treatment is management.
- Kade's Meridian access codes were never revoked. He moved crates through relay points and transfer points and did not ask what was in them.
- Meridian "didn't shut down clean." Multiple sites, spread deliberately, "From themselves."
- The facility: cut fence forced outward "not recently," selective neglect, thin dust, broad faint dust disruptions matching no maintenance route, standby power rising and falling with relays "not synchronized, not random either," a wall calendar stopped three years earlier, a side service door with a dead keypad and a mechanical lock.
- Ch16's payoff: **"It's one."** A single moving point of contact.
- Ch22: Iris is **"closer to Willow than anyone else alive,"** and the entity let her go.
- **The name "Willow" appears only in Ch21 and Ch22, only in narration, and no character ever speaks it.** The prose starts using it at the moment of contact and never explains how it knows.

### 2. The series has to end

Book 3 — The Silence exists in outline and briefs, both of which are now unstable, because they were written against a Book 2 that no longer exists. **Book 3 is open too, but the series still has to arrive somewhere.**

## THE THREE ABANDONED ATTEMPTS, AND WHY

**Read this before proposing a fourth.** Each failed for a specific, diagnosable reason, and the reasons are not the same.

| Attempt | What it was | Why it was abandoned |
|---|---|---|
| **The Hunger** (to 2026-08-04) | Direct sequel. Willow becomes strategic, Lena targeted at the midpoint, Virek surfaces, the origin revealed secondhand in one chapter. | **The origin was reported, not dramatized.** The series' emotional bomb — that Willow was born from grief — was a chapter of a man talking in a cabin. The diagnosis was correct. |
| **The Keeping** (2026-08-04 to 08-26) | Prequel. Marin Halloway, Meridian, twelve chapters of origin plus six of intact pre-Book-1 Iris. | **Three costs.** No forward carry: its entire cast appears in no other book. Dramatic irony as the sole engine across 70,000 words, with the Iris thread explicitly forbidden from carrying tension. And a Book 3 left holding two books of plot. |
| **The sequel with a Recovered thread** (2026-08-26 to 08-27) | Sequel. Origin delivered as seven dramatized Marin chapters Iris recovers off Meridian's drives. Willow becomes a killer. Caleb dies at the midpoint. | **Abandoned 2026-08-27 by author decision**, before any prose was written. Two complete brief sets exist for it. |

**The pattern worth noticing:** attempts 1 and 2 both failed on *where the origin goes*. Attempt 3 solved that problem and was abandoned anyway. Whatever comes next should be explicit about whether the origin is the problem it is trying to solve, or whether that question has been carrying more weight than it deserves for three planning cycles.
