Chapter 17 — Tracking
Iris moved beyond the mapped edge.
The last confirmed point held behind her.
Everything ahead was inferred.
Direction consistent.
No deviation.
She followed it.
Kade’s voice remained in memory.
Don’t go alone.
She had acknowledged it.
She had not followed it.
Not out of resistance.
Not out of urgency.
Constraint.
She was the only one who could track it.
That was sufficient.
The outer zones thinned.
Fewer active routes. Less maintained ground. Buildings spaced wider. Systems less consistent.
Iris adjusted her path without checking the map.
She didn’t need to.
The direction held.
Not visually.
Not logically.
Positionally.
A pull.
Faint.
Consistent.
She stopped.
The sensation increased.
Not pain.
Pressure.
Frontal.
Localized.
Duration: sustained.
No escalation. No resolution.
She moved again.
Her device signaled.
Kade.
She answered.
“Where are you?” he said.
“Outer zone.”
“Be specific.”
Iris looked at the display.
Coordinates present.
She read them.
“You’re outside the last mapped point,” he said.
“Yes.”
“You shouldn’t be.”
“I know.”
Pause.
“Iris.”
“Yes.”
“Go back.”
“No.”
Another pause.
“You’re not equipped to do this alone.”
“Yes.”
“That’s not what I’m saying.”
“I know.”
Kade exhaled.
“Then what are you saying?”
Iris considered the question.
“Direction is consistent,” she said.
“That’s not an answer.”
“It is sufficient.”
Silence.
“You’re following it,” he said.
“Yes.”
“How?”
Iris paused.
Not searching.
Verifying.
“It’s ahead,” she said.
Kade didn’t respond immediately.
“That’s not how tracking works,” he said.
“It is consistent.”
“That’s not what I asked.”
Iris held the silence.
Kade spoke again.
“I’m coming to you.”
“No.”
“Iris.”
“You won’t be able to track it.”
“That’s not the point.”
“It is.”
Kade stopped.
Didn’t argue.
“Send your position,” he said.
“I am.”
“Stay where you are.”
“I won’t.”
The line remained open for a moment longer.
Then closed.
Iris continued.
The pressure increased.
Not in intensity.
In persistence.
It did not fade between steps.
It held.
Continuous.
The buildings changed.
Older construction.
Partial function.
Systems active, but isolated.
No integrated grid.
She moved between them.
The direction narrowed.
Less range.
More exact.
She stopped again.
The pressure shifted.
Slightly left.
She adjusted.
Moved.
It corrected.
Centered.
She continued.
Her movement became incremental.
Shorter distances.
Frequent stops.
Each adjustment refined the direction.
Each step reduced uncertainty.
The city had fallen behind her.
No visible transit.
No pedestrian flow.
Only structures.
And space between them.
She reached a corridor between two long-abandoned facilities.
Narrow.
Contained.
The pressure increased.
Not sharply.
But—
present in a way it hadn’t been before.
She stepped forward.
The sensation held.
She stepped again.
Stronger.
Still not pain.
Still not fear.
She stopped.
She was not following a signal.
Not exactly.
She was following change in herself.
She was measuring proximity through loss.
Iris stood in the corridor.
She was closer.
That was certain.
She checked for fear.
Expected response.
Appropriate to context.
None.
No increase in heart rate.
No hesitation.
No aversion.
She noted the absence.
Duration: sustained.
No change.
She moved again.
The corridor opened into a wider space.
Concrete foundation.
Partial structure overhead.
Exposed.
No active systems.
No visible activity.
She stopped at the edge.
The pressure stabilized.
Maximum sustained level.
No further increase.
It was here.
Not visible.
Not confirmed.
But—
consistent.
Iris stepped forward.
No increase.
She stepped again.
Still no change.
She had reached it.
Or—
it had reached her.
There was something to find.
Not a pattern.
Not a cause.
Something.
She was within range.
Iris did not call Kade.
Did not mark the location.
Did not step back.
She remained.
The pressure held.
The absence did.
She had followed it.
She was here.
She waited.
