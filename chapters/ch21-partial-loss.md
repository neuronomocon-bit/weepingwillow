Chapter 21 — Partial Loss
The distance between them closed.
Not fully.
Enough.
Willow moved first.
A single step.
Measured.
Exact.
Iris did not step back.
The field held her in place.
Not restrained.
Not forced.
Held.
Willow lifted her hand.
The motion was smooth.
Continuous.
No hesitation.
No adjustment.
As if the endpoint had already been determined.
Iris watched it rise.
Tracked the path.
No deviation.
Direct.
She tried to move.
The signal formed.
Did not complete.
Delay.
Extended.
Willow’s hand stopped.
Not touching.
Within range.
Close enough that distance no longer mattered.
The pressure shifted.
Not increased.
Focused.
Iris felt it.
Not on the surface.
Not physical.
Internal.
Alignment adjusting.
Without permission.
Without resistance.
She tried again to step back.
The command registered.
Execution—
partial.
Her foot moved.
A fraction.
Stopped.
Willow’s hand moved the final distance.
Contact.
Light.
Minimal.
Sufficient.
No force.
No impact.
Just—
connection.
The effect was immediate.
Something inside her softened. Dissolved.
Edges blurred.
Definition lost.
Iris held still.
Not by choice.
By limitation.
She tracked it.
The change.
Where it was happening.
Not location.
Function.
A response.
A reflex.
Something automatic.
Something she used—
without thinking.
It faltered.
She tried to access it.
The pathway remained.
The result did not.
The connection weakened.
Faded.
Then—
gone.
Willow did not move.
Her hand remained in place.
No pressure.
No variation.
Only contact.
Iris searched for what had changed.
She found the space.
Clear.
Defined.
The shape of it—
intact.
But the content—
absent.
She reached for it.
Nothing responded.
No trace.
No residual.
Just—
absence.
She understood it had been there.
Recently.
Available.
Used.
Now—
not.
Willow’s head tilted.
A small adjustment.
Observation.
Confirmation.
Iris forced movement.
This time—
it completed.
She stepped back.
The contact broke.
Immediately.
The pressure shifted.
Broadened.
Less focused.
Still present.
But no longer—
precise.
Willow did not follow.
Did not reach again.
She remained where she was.
Watching.
Iris took another step back.
This one easier.
The delay reduced.
Not removed.
Reduced.
She stabilized her position.
Distance re-established.
Minimal.
Sufficient.
She checked herself.
Internally.
Inventory.
Functions present.
Responses available.
One—
missing.
She could feel it.
The absence.
Defined.
Specific.
But—
unrecoverable.
She tried to name it.
No reference.
No label.
Only—
gap.
Willow lowered her hand.
The motion identical.
Smooth.
Complete.
Finished before it began.
No reaction followed.
No escalation.
No pursuit.
The exchange—
complete.
Iris stood across from her.
Less.
Not visibly.
Not externally.
But—
reduced.
She held the space where it had been.
Tested it.
Again.
Nothing.
Like pressing against something that should resist—
and finding no surface.
She knew it mattered.
The system had used it.
Relied on it.
Now—
compensating.
She adjusted.
Without deciding to.
The system corrected.
Continued.
Willow remained still.
No further movement.
No indication of intent.
Only—
observation.
Iris did not move forward.
She understood.
Proximity was enough.
Closer—
would continue it.
Iris looked at her.
Then—
looked inward.
The space remained.
Clean.
Precise.
Empty.
She tried again to recall it.
Not the function.
The feeling.
Nothing formed.
Only the awareness—
that something had been there.
And was not anymore.
Iris stood in the room.
Breathing steady.
System active.
Continuing.
She had entered with more.
She knew that.
She could not specify what.
Only that the total—
had changed.
She turned slightly.
Not away.
Not fully.
Just enough to break alignment.
The pressure shifted with it.
Less direct.
More diffuse.
She held there.
At the edge of continuation.
Willow did not move.
Did not speak.
Did not close the distance again.
The exchange had been sufficient.
Iris remained where she was.
Feeling the absence.
Not what had been lost.
Just—
where it had been.
