## Question mark convention
[[Iris Vale]] and Taken characters use **periods** on dialogue questions. Warm characters use question marks. **Exception:** pre-Taking Caleb in Ch11 uses periods as deliberate foreshadowing. Full-sentence questions take question marks for everyone; short tonal probes keep periods. Dialogue tags use **"said"** not "asked" for flat-affect characters.
