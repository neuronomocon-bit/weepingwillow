## The calendar — set 2026-09-02
**Published Book 1 gives no month and almost no elapsed time** — two instances of *"two days"* in the whole novel and not one month name. **Book 2 therefore sets the series calendar, and it had already drifted once.**

| | When |
|---|---|
| **Book 1 Ch1** — Iris is Taken | **First week of January.** Fixed by *"on the dashboard since January"* and Ch9's *"on the dash for eleven weeks"* at Day 42 |
| **Book 1 Ch10** — the café with Lena | Late January |
| **Book 1 Ch22** — Day 0 | **Second week of February.** Book 1 spans about five weeks |
| **Book 2 Ch1** — Day 21 | Early March |
| **Book 2 Ch2** — Day 24 | Early March. Rourke's ten-week mortality window opens in **late December** |
| **Book 2 Ch9** — Day 42 | Late March |
| **Book 2 Ch22** — Day 74 | Early May |

> [!warning] What this fixed
> Ch2 said Iris's nine-name list was given *"in February"* and also *"ten months ago"*, which
> put the scene in December and threw the bench death outside the ten-week window.
> **February is five weeks back, not ten months.** The correction is the better book: Iris
> flags the man in February, Rourke files it correctly, and he is dead inside five weeks.

**Every other anchor already agreed and none of them moved:** *since the winter* (Ch6, Ch7), *in February* for Joel and for Rourke's tablet (Ch6, Ch7), the map on the wall *since January* (Ch7), and Lena *since January* (Ch9).
