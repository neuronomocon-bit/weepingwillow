# The Death Count — Book 2
**Counted off the locked prose 2026-09-04.**

> [!warning] The book never gives a total, and that is deliberate
> **Iris tries and cannot.** *"She could not total it cleanly."* Her final written figure is a
> range. **Do not put a total on the page in Book 3 either** — the unattributability is the horror.

---

## Two mechanisms, kept apart
Ch7 sets the ladder:

> *Band four was self-preservation gone, **the band that killed people slowly** and the one Rourke's eleven had come out of. Band five was **everything, at once, with the body still running.***

- **Band four** — [[Book 1 - The Absence]]'s partial takings coming due. **She never went back to these people.** They stop eating, they do not restart a medication, they do not return when the infection worsens, **because nothing in them objects to anything**
- **Band five** — a killing on first contact. **The dose has increased**
- **And the return** — she goes back to finish a partial. That is [[Caleb Ward]]

**So seventeen of Rourke's four hundred and sixteen are dead by Ch18** (416 → 404 → 399), plus the Ch6 man outside the cohort, plus Lila, plus three nobody can identify.

## Iris's own figures, as written
- **Ch14, Day 51:** *405 to 409 living partials. Return status unknown.* — **four hundred and nine people and five names**
- **Ch18, Day ~67:** *397 to 403 living partials. Deduplication unresolved. Nine deaths in eleven days, six named, five confirmed in R's set, three unnamed and untested against the above.*

## Why there is no total — three reasons, all in the text
1. **Rourke's summaries are de-identified**, so Joel, Lila and the two from the outer zone cannot be placed inside or beside the 416
2. **Three of the nine have no names at all**
3. *"A count of the living with three dead people standing outside it is not a count."*

And the floor rule she applies: **the top cannot move, because an unnamed death cannot raise it; the bottom comes down by three, because the floor of a range is the number you get when everything you cannot check goes the worst way it could go.**

## The unattributability, which is the point
**Every certificate is correct.** [[Elena Rourke]] could defend all eleven to a review board **without lying once.** No signature, no toxicology, no anatomical finding any two of them share.

Her Ch18 finding: **prior flattening.** *"Every death I can put a name to was already in my caseload."* And immediately the limit on it: *"It isn't a predictor… What I have got is a finding about where to look. It says nothing whatsoever about who."*

## The other thread
**[[Marin Halloway]], [[B2 Ch20 - The Last Session]]** — consumed entire, at Year −3, **and she consented.** Chronologically that precedes everything above.

> [!info] A rough order of magnitude, and it is inference rather than the book's number
> **On the order of twenty-odd deaths across the eleven weeks**, of which **only two are
> dramatized on the page.** That figure sums numbers the prose deliberately keeps in separate
> windows. **It is not a canon number and must not become one.**
