## The three decisions of 2026-09-02, in more detail

**1. Narration contractions — match Book 2.**
Book 2's narration runs 98–100% expanded; Book 1's runs 50–75%. **Book 2's own register is the standard.** The hundred-odd Act I sentences stay as written. **Dialogue is untouched and runs the other way, as it must.**

**2. Length — accept ~70k.**
The old 78,000–85,000 came from **dividing a target**; the briefs describe roughly 3,280-word chapters and always did. **The per-chapter budgets stay in the briefs as a ceiling and a diagnostic, not a target.** A chapter at 87% with all its beats is finished. **This licenses neither padding nor trimming — a chapter is not too long because the target moved.**

**3. The Ch8 identifier — resolved by a third option neither original reached.**
**MN-R-0338 was issued twice**: recycled off a veterans'-cohort course that closed three years earlier, with the closed record left sitting on the live index because records never finished the housekeeping.

**Willow's string never changes**, so the reader carries one token from Ch4 to the end, **and Ch15 still earns its three checks**, because what Iris has to establish is the number's *history* rather than the number. **Nothing about it is ever stated.**
