# Arc History

## Arc 1 — "The Hunger" (retired)
Book 2 was **a direct sequel called The Hunger.**

## First pivot — 2026-08-04 — the prequel
It became **a prequel** dramatizing Willow's origin.

**The diagnosis was correct:** the series' emotional bomb was being **reported** — one chapter of [[Simon Virek]] talking in a cabin — rather than dramatized.

## Second pivot — 2026-08-26 — CURRENT
**The prequel was dropped. Book 2 is a sequel again**, and the origin is delivered as **fully dramatized [[Marin Halloway]] chapters that [[Iris Vale]] recovers off Meridian's drives.**

> [!info] This keeps the dramatization and discards the prequel's three costs
> 1. **No forward carry** — its entire cast appeared in no other book
> 2. **Dramatic irony as the sole engine** across 70,000 words
> 3. **A Book 3 left carrying two books of plot**

**The same pivot made [[Willow]] a killer.**

## The seven decisions of the second pivot
1. Book 2 is a **sequel** with the origin recovered off the drives
2. **Willow becomes a killer** because her protocol is failing
3. **Satisfaction, not pleasure**
4. **Her face resolves toward [[Willow Halloway]]'s** — and [[Simon Virek]] is the only living person who can name it
5. **Book 3 ends with the compulsion trap, then the water**
6. **Iris ends ambiguous**
7. **[[Caleb Ward]] dies at Book 2's midpoint**

**The title is "The Ending."**

## What was archived rather than patched
Both superseded arcs live in `series-bible/archive/`, **organised by when they died** — `arc-01-hunger/`, `arc-02-prequel/`, `arc-03-superseded/`, `drafts/`, `superseded-tooling/`. Read `archive/README.md` first.

**Book 3's prequel-era brief set was archived rather than patched, because eight separate things in it were wrong.**

> it used to be.**

## Tooling that died along the way
**Book 1's prose was drafted by ChatGPT.** As of Book 2 it was dropped — **it could not reliably hold the prose hard rules** — and Claude drafts the chapters directly.

The ChatGPT system prompt is archived. **It duplicated the prose rules, which is how one copy goes stale.** `03-themes-and-tone.md` is now the only source for them.

**The staging file died the same way.** Book 1 kept a review file holding the whole running manuscript *alongside* the individual chapter files, so every fix had to be applied twice. **Two copies is how one of them silently goes stale.** There is now no staging file, and `chapters-book2/NN-slug.md` is the single source of truth.

> [!warning] The tradeoff, stated plainly
> `build-manuscript.js` picks up every numbered file in `chapters-book2/`, so an in-progress
> draft is inside the export path. **Check what the build reports before exporting anything.**

## The one number that keeps going stale
**Six is the age of the program**, from Willow Halloway's intake at Year −6. **It is not the age of anything else.** Three separate documents carried a wrong six — the Ch5 brief, [[Minor Characters|Pell]]'s entry, and the Ch7 brief — and all three were corrected. See [[Master Timeline]].
