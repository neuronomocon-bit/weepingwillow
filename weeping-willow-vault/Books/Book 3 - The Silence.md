## Where it starts
Weeks after [[Book 2 - The Ending]]. [[Willow]] becomes **strategic** and **no longer needs to touch anyone** from the Act I turn — which is the point of removing the contact requirement: Book 1's tension was *don't let her touch you*, a solvable problem, and once it is gone there is nowhere left to stand.

## The shape
- The city misdiagnoses the spread, then **welcomes it**
- [[Lena Vale]] is partially Taken at the midpoint
- [[Elena Rourke]] breaks in public, then does institutional damage
- [[Daniel Kade]] attempts containment alone and is partially Taken
- [[Simon Virek]] is found — **the only living person who ever saw [[Willow Halloway]]** — and he recognizes the face
- [[Iris Vale]] opens herself completely

## The ending
**The compulsion trap, then the water.** Willow reaches the irreducible core, cannot take it, cannot stop, and **the attempt gives her what she has been assembling toward for six years.** She finishes becoming Willow Halloway, walks past Iris, and goes into the water.

**Iris does not kill her. Iris finishes her.**

## Its engine
Dramatic irony, reshaped by the pivot. The reader finishes Book 2 knowing what Willow is — **and so does Iris**, who recovered the whole origin and felt none of it. What she never reaches is **why Lena**.

> [!warning] Do not let the prose reach for the reader's knowledge
> If a sentence would collapse for someone who had not read Book 2, it is doing the reader's job.

## What Book 2 hands it, and what it forbids
- **Lena is located, not approached.** She is never in physical danger in Book 2
- **Willow's face is complete and Virek can name it** — no character in Book 2 recognizes it
- **What Ch22 leaves in her is a value that will not close and a system that cannot leave a value open.** It has **no name in her.** The prose has never called it anger, frustration or want, and **Book 3 has to earn any word it uses for it**
- **Every recovered system stops at the seal.** Everything anyone knows about the sealed years is inference. This constrains Book 3 as hard as it constrained Book 2 — see [[The Substrate]]

## The public-awareness shift
Book 1's horror lever was *nobody believes it is happening.* Book 3's is **everybody can see it and they are grateful.**

*Act I–II:* numbers too big to ignore, explanation completely wrong. Media frames it as an epidemic — neurological, environmental, viral. A task force forms and the wrong solutions are formally adopted. Iris knows and cannot prove it, and her deterioration makes her less credible every month.

*Act III:* the metrics improve. Volatility down, friction down, incidents down. Health systems register it as recovery and policy calls it progress. **The world never fights back properly, because the damage does not look like damage.**
