## The series in one line each

| Book | The question | The answer |
|---|---|---|
| **One — The Absence** | *What was taken from me?* | Her love for one specific person, and she is not the only one |
| **Two — The Ending** | *Where did it come from, and why is it killing now?* | A grieving sister, a dead sister's map, and a method that is failing |
| **Three — The Silence** | *What happens when the damage looks like improvement?* | A city that stops objecting, and a woman who finishes her |

**Reading order is publication order, and chronological order now matches it.**
The Absence → The Ending → The Silence.

## The shape of it

**Book 1 shows the subtraction. Book 2 shows where the subtractor came from and gives it a body count. Book 3 shows a city deciding it prefers being subtracted.** The threat escalates on parallel axes so the books cannot repeat each other — see [[The Escalation Ladder]].

> [!warning] The rules that outlive the books
> **Nobody in this series is a villain.** Not Willow, not Marin, not Rourke, not Raman.
> The horror runs on everyone behaving reasonably.
> **Nobody ever explains the name.** Iris *finds* it. That is not the same thing.
> **The entity has no appetite.** She is finishing, not feeding.
