# The Taking
**[[Willow]]'s primary mechanism. The horror of the series is subtraction, not violence.**

---

## The mechanism
- **Requires direct physical contact**, usually skin to skin. True through Books 1 and 2; removed at Book 3's Act I turn
- The initial touch is **calm and non-violent**
- The target experiences sudden stillness and flash-like emotional recall
- She identifies **high-density emotional nodes**
- **She removes the emotional charge first, then the associated memory structures**
- **Not clean.** Always collateral loss: identity damage, gaps, distortions, emotional flattening
- Duration: seconds to minutes, depending on resistance
- What she takes becomes part of her internal system, used for behavioral modeling, speech patterns, and emotional simulation

## What it looks like from inside — Book 1 Ch12
**Feeling separates from image, then image follows.** The final thing taken is not an event but a state: **the sense that things mattered.**

Afterward: *"Nothing in him marked the change. Nothing in him noted the absence."*

## What it looks like from outside
Calm. Present. Functional. Makes eye contact, answers questions, goes through the motions. **The surface is intact and the interior is gone.**

The register of a Taken person: *"I know what is necessary." · "It was appropriate." · "I respond when required." · "I don't have that information."*

> [!info] Question mark convention
> [[Iris Vale]] and Taken characters use **periods** on dialogue questions — flat affect.
> Warm characters ([[Elena Rourke]], [[Daniel Kade]], [[Lena Vale]], Leah, pre-Taking Caleb)
> use question marks. **Exception:** pre-Taking Caleb in Book 1 Ch11 uses periods as
> deliberate foreshadowing. Full-sentence questions take question marks for everyone;
> short tonal probes (*"How." / "Why." / "Pain."*) keep periods.

## The passive field — the presence effect
When she is nearby, people feel **calm and detached.** Sound perception dulls slightly. Reaction times slow. **Subtle, never obvious.**

For Iris this is inverted into a proximity sense: pressure in the head, disorientation, flattening intensifying. *"Location: frontal. Duration: brief. Intensity: minimal."*

> [!warning] Part of Iris does not want to fight it. The calm feels like relief.

## Her perception
She does not see like humans. She perceives **emotional intensity, memory density, cognitive activity patterns**, and is drawn to people with strong unresolved emotional structures.

## The two escalations — discovered in this order, never conflated
- **The dose.** She takes more from each new subject, because taking less has resolved nothing in eighteen months. Discovered Book 2 Ch6, established as a curve in Ch7. **This is what kills**
- **The return.** She goes back to subjects whose partial results were unsatisfactory **and finishes them.** Discovered Ch11, understood Ch14. **This is what makes every Taken person in the published book an open job**

## The deaths she causes without touching anyone
Taken people die **because what was removed was keeping them alive.** No fear, no self-preservation, no reason to seek treatment. They stop eating, they walk into traffic, they do not present for care.

**[[Willow Halloway]] drowned.** That mechanism scales to a city-wide mortality curve nobody can attribute.
