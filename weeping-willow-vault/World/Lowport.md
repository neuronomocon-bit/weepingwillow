# Lowport, Maine
**Fictional coastal industrial city. ~2050. The only setting the series has.**

---

## Identity
A former economic hub, partially declining. Still functioning, but uneven. **It existed before the tech and will exist after it.**

## Geography
- Dense inner city
- Outer industrial zones
- Shoreline infrastructure: shipyards, container terminals, energy plants, water treatment
- Older underground systems — drainage, decommissioned transit, utility tunnels
- Mixed-income zones with areas that feel **forgotten but still powered**

## Atmosphere
Constant damp air, from proximity to water rather than for style. Fog-heavy mornings. Salt corrosion everywhere. Rust, aging materials, patched systems. Older districts that never got updated.

**Cold. Always slightly cold.**

> [!info] Weather and environment are constant presences, not set dressing

## State of the world
**Functioning, but strained.** Not collapse, not dystopian chaos. Systems still run, people go to work, life continues. But cracks are visible, services degrade in certain areas, some zones feel under-maintained and quietly abandoned, and infrastructure ages faster than it is replaced.

**Key feeling: everything still works. Just not as well as it should.**

## Technology
**Everyday and visible** — advanced wearables, smart contacts, discreet overlays, voice systems and ambient AI, predictive logistics and policing and health monitoring. **Walk down the street and it still feels like our world.**

**Rare and specialized** — implants exist but are not widespread. Mostly medical or specialist professions. **A tool, not a lifestyle choice.**

**Neural and memory tech is the hidden layer.** Not public-facing, not consumer tech. It exists in research programs, corporate black projects, and specialized medical trials. **The public does not understand what this tech can actually do.** Capabilities: memory mapping, emotional pattern extraction, neural data storage and replication. Status: experimental → partially deployed → quietly buried.

> [!warning] Tone rule
> **Grounded language. No stylized cyberpunk vocabulary.** Technology is practical, quiet
> and lived-in, never spectacle. Everything should feel like it could exist within 20–30 years.

## The facility
[[Meridian Neurological]] still stands on the coast. **Sealed, not demolished.** Locals avoid the area without quite knowing why.
