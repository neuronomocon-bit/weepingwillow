# Dr. Marin Halloway
**The deviation. POV of Book 2's seven Recovered chapters — 4, 8, 10, 13, 15, 17, 20.**
**[[Willow Halloway]]'s older sister. The person who removed the limiter.**

> [!warning] She does not appear in Books 1 or 3 and is never named in either
> Not the protagonist of Book 2 — [[Iris Vale]] is. **Marin is the protagonist of the thing Iris is recovering.**

---

## The critical writing rule
> [!warning] There is no scene in which Marin does the wrong thing
> No villainy, no hubris, no warning ignored for effect, no moment where the music changes.
> **If any chapter of hers reads as a horror movie about a scientist, it has failed.**

**Clarified, because getting this wrong in either direction breaks the book.** *"Never does the wrong thing"* governs **how the prose stages her, not whether she is ethically flawless.** She is not:

- the consent conversation in Ch4 is **not adequate informed consent**
- the research identifier **does not de-identify** Willow from Marin
- the deviation in Ch17 is **undocumented**

**All three are intentional and load-bearing.** The gate is that **every one of them is what a competent, decent person does under that specific pressure**, and the prose never stages it as a lapse. Marin giving her frightened sister the plain version of an answer instead of the version with the intervals in it **is what any decent clinician does**, and it is also the thing that turns out to matter.

**Show the choice. Say nothing about it.**

**The test:** if a reader can see the failure and cannot find a moment where Marin behaved badly, the gate is holding. **If the prose points at the failure, it has broken.**

## Personality
Competent, precise, unhurried. Good at her job and quietly proud of it. **Not arrogant, not reckless, not ambitious — she is the least dramatic person in the series.**

**Her tell:** she reaches for procedure when she is in pain, and talks about her sister, and later her grief, in clinical register. **The prose must never point this out.**

## Her sister
She recommended the program. She sat on the wrong side of the glass during the sessions. **She watched it work.**

Then Willow drowned, and Marin has **no way to determine whether the treatment she recommended is what let her walk into the water**, and no data set in the world can answer it.

## The descent — every step smaller than the one before
- **(Ch15)** Signs the closure form. Does not purge the baseline, which is research data, which is kept. **Constructs the technicality while standing there, in about nine seconds, and it is airtight, and she knows exactly what it is**
- **(Ch15)** Opens it eleven days later. Read-only. Confirms the index is complete and closes it again
- **(Ch17)** Renders it. Then runs it on the substrate, sandboxed and isolated, because the substrate renders at a fidelity nothing else has
- **(Ch17)** Adds one research identifier to the integration limiter's **exclusion list**, which the limiter has supported for six years and which is in the documentation. Moves the **autonomy constraint from two to four**, a documented value in use at two other sites. **Two parameters, one afternoon, undocumented.** Then coffee, three emails, and a procurement meeting she contributes usefully to
- **(Ch17, closing page)** Runs it. **Something comes back that is not playback.** She stops recording the session
- *(record)* Covers the consumption discrepancies
- *(record)* Says nothing when a technician is Taken, because saying it means termination
- *(record, surfaced by Iris in Ch14)* **Reclassifies the substrate from transferable asset to fixed site infrastructure**, three weeks before the seal, so it stays with the building. **This is why it was still in there when Iris cut through the fence.** Iris finds the decision and not the name — the signature is on the degraded index — and **she never learns it was Marin**
- **(Ch20)** Comes back after the seal and **opens the channel both directions**

> [!info] Why Ch17 breaks no rule
> Both parameter changes are **documented features used as designed.** The clinical
> configuration is untouched and no patient is exposed. Neither goes into change control
> because change control covers the clinical configuration — **that is scope, not judgment.**
> **The gate holds because there is no rule to break.**

## Her fate
**Consumed, entire and consented** (Ch20). It took the charge, the structure, the reasoning, and the grief.

Her last awareness is that it is doing exactly what the program was designed to do, correctly and gently, and that **the relief is enormous. She is happy. Do not undercut it.**

Her grief is integrated into [[Willow]] and **is the reason Willow believes she is helping.**

> [!warning] Ruled 2026-09-03 — she says her sister's name in Ch20 and the word is not on the page
> Withholding it preserves the distinction that **she says her sister's name and does not
> address the entity**, and lets the reader supply the word without the prose collapsing
> those two meanings. It also fits the chapter's documentary logic: **the one word absent
> from every form is absent from the page too.** Quoting would be more immediately dramatic
> and **less exact, and exactness matters more here.**
