# Dr. Elena Rourke
**Behavioral health authority for [[Lowport]]. The skeptic, and never the fool.**

> [!warning] She is wrong for the right reasons
> Dismissive of unknown-entity explanations **not out of stupidity, but out of professional
> framework.** She genuinely believes she is helping. **She is never written as an obstacle
> who is simply being difficult.**

---

## Core belief
> *"This is neurological. Environmental. Treatable."*

## Her data
Hundreds of cases classified as **behavioral flattening, dissociative presentation, emotional attenuation.** Neural imaging shows reduced activity in specific emotional processing centers, **not uniform, targeted.** **No cases recover fully; treatment is management.**

In Book 1 Ch6 she detects [[Iris Vale]] presenting with the same markers — flattened affect, delayed response, reduced emotional variance — and gives her redacted case summaries on a tablet. **Pragmatic, not an alliance.**

## Her arc
- **Book 1** — rejects Iris entirely. Her own data, her own framework, her own conclusions. Book 1 Ch14: *"You used to elaborate." / "The data doesn't require elaboration."* Her steadiness tightens rather than cracks
- **Book 2 Act I (Ch2)** — her caseload starts producing **deaths her category does not predict**, and no cause of death fits, **because the cause was removed months ago.** She gives Iris the curve — not the files, the curve — and a threshold: bring back something that predicts a death before it happens and they will have a different conversation. **This is not an alliance. It is a scientist who has run out of better ideas and is being professional about it**
- **Book 2 Ch12 — she cracks and does not break.** [[Caleb Ward]]'s body has a mechanism she can describe and no cause she can name. She stops *defending* the position: she gives Iris everything, off any record, **and says plainly that she will deny it.** That is her entire movement in Book 2, **and this book must leave her something to break**
- **Book 2 Ch18** — reads a statement to a public meeting that she stopped believing in Ch12
- **Book 3 Act I** — stands on a panel and reads the wrong answer to a city, and does it well
- **Book 3 Act II turn** — **breaks in public.** Brings everything, including the city-level data: volatility down, friction down, incidents down. **The curve reads as recovery and is the shape of a population being emptied**
- **Book 3 Act III** — does the institutional thing at the worst possible moment and brings people to the confrontation. **They die.** She survives it
- **Book 3 ending** — still working. Treating what she can, documenting what she cannot, **including what she did**

## Voice note
Her dialogue runs **heavily contracted** — measured at **7% expanded forms** in published Book 1. A Book 2 draft had drifted to **61%**, which is a continuity defect against a published book. It now sits at **21%**, and the residual is her recitation register. See [[Audit Method]].

**She uses question marks.** Warm characters do; [[Iris Vale]] and Taken characters use periods on dialogue questions.
