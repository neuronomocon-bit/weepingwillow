# Lila Mercer
**The first fully Taken person [[Iris Vale]] interacts with directly.**
**Book 1 Ch4 · Book 2 Ch14 · dies in Book 2 Ch18, one line, secondhand.**

---

## Behavior
Calm. Present. Functional. Makes eye contact, responds to questions, goes through the motions. **Completely hollow beneath.**

Her register: *"I don't have that information." · "I respond when required." · "It is appropriate."* **Use one, not all of them.**

## Function in Book 1
Shows the outcome clearly and early — **establishes what "Taken" looks like before the reader sees it happen.** Sets the tone: not violent, not dramatic, **just absence.**

## Book 2 Ch14 — the un-warnable scene
Iris, having worked out that the entity **returns to finish partial results**, goes to Lila's flat to warn her.

**Lila cannot be warned.** Iris explains it clearly and completely: something is coming back for you, it will not hurt, you will not object, **and that is the problem.** Lila understands the words, agrees the information is useful, and asks a reasonable clarifying question about scheduling.

Same building, same second floor, same even hallway light. She agrees to the visit through the door panel without asking why, **because nothing in her objects to anything, including a visit.** She listens the whole way through and **does not do any of the things a person does with their face while they wait for a turn to speak.**

> *"That is useful information." / "Thank you for bringing it."*
> She repeats the warning back in order with nothing missing and asks **"Is there a date."**
> Told the intervals cannot be narrowed: **"Then I will continue as normal. I respond when required."**

**Nothing in her objects to anything, which is precisely what makes her impossible to protect.** Iris sits in that flat understanding that **she has just described her own position to a mirror.**

## The inverted physical response — never named

> In Book 1 Ch4, in January, Iris's arms and shoulders tightened in that chair and she sat
> in the van afterward until it let go.
> In Ch14 she runs the same check, waits, and **nothing comes up.**
> She records location, duration and absence.

## Her death
**Ch18 — one line, secondhand, the fourth name in a list of six inside the escalation.** No scene, no return to the flat. **She does not die on the page.**
