# Daniel Kade
**Ally and handler. Former [[Meridian Neurological]] logistics and site coordination.**
**Not a scientist. Knows something went wrong, and does not know what came out.**

---

## Function
Hires [[Iris Vale]] for the initial Meridian recovery job, feeds her locations and subsequent work, and is the primary connection between Iris and the institutional side of the cover-up.

## Personality
Practical. Controlled. **Does not speculate, acts.** Does what needs doing without dramatizing it.

**Voice:** warm, and he uses question marks. **He never delivers exposition in a monologue.** Information comes out of him in pieces while his hands are busy.

## His guilt, and the shape of it
> [!info] It is never dramatic. It is logistical.
> He moved boxes to locations he did not ask about, and **the worst thing he ever did was
> write down the answer he was given.**

Book 1 Ch8: *"I moved things through here. Crates. Equipment. Data units. Didn't ask what was in them." "Didn't ask why."*

He recognized the site types from his own work — **relay points** (data movement) and **transfer points** (equipment).

## His arc
- **Book 1** — contained and procedural. Hires Iris for **MN-44-7**. Hints at Meridian's scope: multiple sites, primary, secondary storage, offsite data mirrors, six confirmed adjacent locations. **His access codes still work; clearance was never revoked**
- **Book 2 Ch3** — **opens the post-shutdown files.** A rented lockup, the crates he moved, the manifests, and the sibling contracts to the job he sent her on. **He is asking to be forgiven and calling it work, and Iris hears the request accurately and has nothing to answer it with.** He tells her about a row of crates he was asked about twice, because the manifest was ambiguous and he did not want to get it wrong — he said secondary, wrote it down, moved them, and did not ask what was in them
- **Book 2 Ch14** — he is the one who says the frightening obvious thing out loud **so Iris does not have to**
- **Book 3** — full partner from page one. Goes back to the transfer points on his own time. **Objects, hard, to Iris using herself as equipment.** Attempts containment alone and is **partially Taken** on the page for it. Survives, functional and quieter, carrying the old guilt and the new reduction together, **describing his own reasons in a voice that no longer contains them**
