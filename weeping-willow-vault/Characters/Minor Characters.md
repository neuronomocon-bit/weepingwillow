# Minor Characters
**The people who make [[Meridian Neurological]] a real workplace, and the first victims.**

> [!warning] The horror depends on nobody in this building doing anything wrong
> Every one of these people is competent and behaves reasonably. **That is the point.**

---

## Pell — substrate technician, night rotation
**The first Taken person in the world. He never knows.**

Goes into the lab for a routine check and makes contact. Comes out functional, correct, and pleasant, **and finishes his shift.**

Under the sequel structure he is **never dramatized.** He arrives as an internal incident record Iris surfaces in **Book 2 Ch7**: his supervisor's note, dated two days later, observing that **he had stopped being difficult about the things he was always difficult about.** The note is not a complaint. It reads as mild approval, and it was written by a man filling in a shift report.

> **The first Taken person in the world is a line in a performance file, and it is the nicest thing anyone ever wrote about him.**

**He is the second half of Book 2's Act I turn:** the dosage curve has an origin point, it is inside Meridian, and it runs continuously from a Tuesday **three and a half years ago** to a body on the north side two days ago.

> [!warning] Dating — corrected 2026-08-31, do not revert it
> **Three and a half years, not six.** Pell cannot precede the deviation (before it, the
> limiter is in place and autonomy is set to two, so a taking is not possible). He precedes
> the shutdown and **causes it to move**: Raman's wind-down was to complete the following
> spring, and Pell brings it forward by several months. That puts him at roughly **Year −3.4**.
> The gap to Rourke's earliest case is **about eighteen months, not three years.**
> **Six is the age of the program**, from Willow Halloway's intake at Year −6. It is not the age of anything Pell did.

Not in Books 1 or 3.

## Dr. Feld — [[Willow Halloway]]'s treating clinician
On the clinical file that [[Marin Halloway]] **deliberately kept herself off.**

Thorough, slow, hard to rush, **cold on paper and warm in person.** Marin read six years of her outcome data and asked two people she trusted what Feld was like in a room, **none of which was proper, and did it anyway.**

She knows Marin observes from the other side of the glass **and does not stop her.** Her first line to Marin about it is **"You'll hate it,"** and then she goes in and starts the session.

**She is excellent at her job and does nothing wrong.** Book 2 Ch8, 13, 15, 20. Not in Books 1 or 3.

## Terri Roth — reception and intake
Runs the front desk. Reading glasses up in her hair, two screens angled away from the counter, **dry and quick and good at her job.** She has worked there since before the clinical wing was finished.

**Function: the ordinary institutional life of the building.** Meridian has to read as a real workplace with real people in it, because the horror depends on nobody in it doing anything wrong. **Terri is the load-bearing proof of that.**

- **Ch4** — books Willow in. **Gets a real laugh out of her inside ninety seconds.** [[Simon Virek]] hears that laugh from down the corridor, **which is why he can recognize her face six years later**
- **Ch8** — knows Willow by name by the fourth week and has a running thing with her at the desk. Twelve weeks of a person being seen, weekly, by an institution that is helping her. **Keep it warm**
- **Ch15** — has the closure paperwork ready when Marin comes in, and says something ordinary and kind. **The last time anyone in that building treats Marin as a bereaved sister rather than a colleague.** Four lines, and do not signal it
- **Ch17** — the car-in-the-lot-early line, which clocks the frequency of Marin's visits

> [!warning] She never learns anything, never suspects anything, and is never used to deliver information
> **She is a person who worked at a place.** Not in Books 1 or 3.

## Dr. Raman — program director
Runs the Meridian program. Competent, busy, and **correct in every scene.**

**Ch8, on the residue:** the decay model was fitted on the veterans' cohort at a third to a half of current session loads, the expansion cases run heavier, and **a load-dependent tail treated as fixed produces a shelf.** It explains the observation, names a mechanism, and makes a testable prediction. **Nobody in that scene is wrong.**

**Ch19, on Virek's ticket, read six years later by Iris:** the funding failed, the site is consolidating, everything in the substrate is scheduled for termination when the wind-down completes next spring. **All of that is true.**

His review threshold is **1%**.

> [!warning] Do not write Raman as an obstacle or a company man
> **The reason nobody stops this is that everyone involved is behaving reasonably.**

## The Ch6 victim — the first over-taking
Unnamed, **and stays unnamed.** A man on the north side who was fine yesterday, found sitting down, who answered the first responders correctly and **stopped answering some hours later. The stopping has no event in it.**

He is the first death that is unambiguously a killing: **everything removed at once, on a first visit.** He is not in Rourke's caseload and was never partially Taken.

> [!warning] He has a name and the reader never gets it
> The neighbor gives it to Iris, she writes it down, and she uses it once — to send it to
> Rourke and get back one word. **Do not put it on the page and do not give him a scene.**
> Iris arrives in time to be told what he said, not in time to hear it.

## Joel and Leah — Book 1 Ch3
**Joel** is the first reported anomaly case Iris investigates. A dock worker on Lowport's north side, case ref **K-01**, flagged by [[Daniel Kade]]. Gone for two days, returned "fine," functional but hollow. His responses as written: *"I know what is necessary." · "It was appropriate."*

His case is what triggers Iris's report to Kade: **"Something has been removed."**

**Leah** is his wife. She shows the human cost from the other side — confused, frightened, unable to explain what changed. **She mirrors what [[Lena Vale]] experiences with Iris, at a smaller scale.**
