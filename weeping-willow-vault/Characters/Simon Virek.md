# Dr. Simon Virek
**The witness. Meridian substrate researcher, adjacent to [[Marin Halloway]]'s work.**
**Not the one who caused it. The one who saw it and let it go.**

---

## Status
Disappeared after the shutdown. **Alive, hiding, worn thin.** Found in Book 3.

## Personality
Careful. Observant. Slightly pedantic. **Unhurried in a way that reads as thoroughness and is partly avoidance.** Carries guilt — not for what he did, but for what he did not stop.

## What solves him
> [!warning] He is the only living person who ever saw [[Willow Halloway]]
> He worked in that building for the twelve weeks she came in weekly. He passed her in the
> corridor. **He heard her make [[Minor Characters|Terri Roth]] laugh at the front desk.**
> Everyone else who saw her is dead, scattered by the shutdown, or unfindable.

So when Iris shows him what is walking around [[Lowport]], **he recognizes the face.** He is the only person alive who can say whose it is.

**That is the payoff Book 2's seven Recovered chapters exist to earn:** the reader spent a book with that woman, and now watches a man come apart over her face, **while Iris stands there unable to feel what he is feeling.**

He also tells Iris what Willow Halloway was like. **Iris cannot use one word of it.**

## His arc
- **Book 2 Ch8** — raises the substrate residue observation **the way a person raises something they expect to be told is nothing.** Told tolerance is tolerance. Accepts it, because [[Minor Characters|Raman]]'s answer is a good answer and there is no second observation to stack on it. Stays late to re-run the decay curve, gets the same result, and goes home
- **Book 2 Ch19, as a record and not a scene** — Iris surfaces his escalation ticket. Unaccounted integration events, an autonomy constraint that does not match its logged value, **raised once, six years ago.** The program director's response is on the file and is entirely reasonable. **The ticket is closed by the man who raised it, with no complaint. That is the whole record.** A man noticed, asked once, got a good answer, and stopped
- **Book 3 Ch10** — a trace in the recovered data. **Breadcrumbs left deliberately, structured for someone who would understand them.** Iris is that person and he has never heard of her
- **Book 3 Ch12** — found. Alive, hiding, worn thin, pedantic even now. Delivers a partial, slightly inaccurate account like a deposition, and supplies the vulnerability: **the system requires coherence**

## His shelf
The residual coherence figure that will not move: **0.4% of session load**, steady across nineteen and then thirty-one sessions, against a tolerance of 2% and a model predicting under 0.1%.

He ran the exclusions in order: sampling head swap, manual flush, clean cycle with no patient material.

> [!warning] The shelf is not evidence of limiter failure and may never be used as such
> It is explained by **a load-dependent tail treated as fixed.** Raman is right. See [[The Substrate]].

## Writing rule
> [!warning] The ticket is a closed ticket. Do not make it poignant.
> **Raman's answer stays correct.** The horror is that everyone behaved reasonably.
