# Lena Vale
**[[Iris Vale]]'s younger sister. The emotional anchor Iris can no longer feel.**

> [!warning] Do not make her precious and do not make her a saint
> **She is irritating in the specific way of someone who is right.**

---

## Personality
Warm. Direct. Persistent. Refuses to accept that Iris has changed, and still fights for the relationship.

## The key dynamic
Lena still loves Iris completely. **Iris remembers Lena and feels nothing.** Lena calls out the change and refuses to let it go.

**She represents exactly what is missing** — which is why she is the thing the entity eventually finds.

## Book 1 — Ch10
A café off a residential street. Lena pushes; Iris performs. The exchange the chapter exists for:

> *"I love you." / "I love you."*
> Words matched. Tone aligned. No delay. Lena's expression: **uncertain, searching, almost convinced. Then not.**

She had already consulted a doctor, described Iris's behavior, and been told stress or dissociation. Her list of what changed: *"You used to push back. You used to argue with me. You used to care if I was annoyed with you."*

## Book 2 — Ch9, she stops asking and starts arriving
At **Iris's apartment**, an evening that runs longer than Iris planned for. She has driven over, brought food, and is **not leaving on the schedule Iris built the evening around.** She is doing what people do when they have decided presence is the only remaining instrument.

Iris performs, competently, and **is better at it than she was in Book 1 Ch10. Lena catches it anyway** — not from any single line, but from the aggregate. And she does not leave.

**The beat the chapter exists for:** Lena says the true thing plainly and without accusation. She has been talking to someone who answers correctly for months, and she has decided **she would rather have the person who answers correctly than nothing.** She is choosing the diminished version, out loud, and she is at peace with it.

Iris can identify exactly what that costs, and can identify that a person would be undone by hearing it, and constructs the response. **It is the best work she does in the book and it is entirely fabricated, and the fabrication is not cruelty. It is the only available form of care.**

Nothing is resolved and nothing is confessed. **Lena stays the night on the couch without asking, which is not a question Iris can decline.** From Ch9 on she is inside the situation and close.

## Book 2 — Ch22, she is not in the chapter and the chapter is about her
During the failed second visit, [[Willow]] reaches **the empty place where Iris's love for her sister used to be and stops there**, because it is the only part of the structure that matches anything. She examines the shape of what is missing, **which is the shape of a younger sister**, and she is looking at it when she stops.

> [!warning] Lena is never approached in Book 2 and is never in physical danger in it
> **What happens is that she is located.** Iris registers it as a duration and never works
> out what it was. **The reader carries it into Book 3 alone.**

## Book 3
**Partially Taken at the midpoint.** Diminished, not hollowed. Her persistence is both heartbreaking and dangerous: it keeps her close to Iris, which keeps her close to Willow.
