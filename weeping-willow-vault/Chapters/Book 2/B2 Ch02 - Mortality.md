# Book 2 · Chapter 2 — Mortality
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Day 24** · **2,951 words** · **LOCKED**
**Location:** Lowport Public Health. [[Elena Rourke]]'s office, and a records room she has no reason to be in

---

## Goal
**Change the threat.** Taken people are dying, and **no cause of death fits, because the cause was removed months ago.**

## The outcome
Rourke gives her **the curve. Not the files, the curve** — and a threshold: **bring back something that predicts a death before it happens and they will have a different conversation.**

> [!info] This is not an alliance
> **It is a scientist who has run out of better ideas and is being professional about it.**

## The emotional beat
Rourke, unprompted, asks how Iris is doing, **and means it clinically**, and Iris can see the assessment being run on her while it happens. She answers accurately. Rourke writes something down. **Iris asks what she wrote and Rourke says she will tell her when there is enough of it to be worth telling.**

## The calendar fix this chapter forced
The draft said Iris's nine-name list was given *"in February"* and also *"ten months ago"*, which put the scene in December **and threw the bench death outside the ten-week window.**

**February is five weeks back, not ten months.** The correction is the better book: **Iris flags the man in February, Rourke files it correctly, and he is dead inside five weeks.** See [[Master Timeline]].
