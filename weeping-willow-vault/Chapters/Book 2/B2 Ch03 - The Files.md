## Goal
Open the drives, and **establish the two classes of media that the rest of the book runs on.**

## The two classes — this is the investigation spine
1. **The research archive.** Intact, or intact enough. Baseline maps, session data, substrate logs, facilities reconciliation. **Everything filed under research identifiers, not names.** All seven Recovered chapters come from here
2. **The clinical and administrative index.** The set that maps identifiers to patients. **Badly degraded** — out of a wet transfer point, written to the cheapest media in the building, and the last thing anyone cared about while a company was dissolving

## The emotional beat
**Kade is asking to be forgiven and calling it work**, and Iris can see the structure of it exactly and has nothing to offer against it.

He tells her about a row of crates he was asked about **twice**, because the manifest was ambiguous and he did not want to get it wrong. **He said secondary, wrote it down, moved them, and did not ask what was in them.**

## The Book 1 payoff added in the audit
The chapter owed the **deliberate site-spread** behind Book 1 Ch5's *"From themselves."* It was added **as evidence, not dialogue**, and it is now the reason the archive and the index were separated at all.
