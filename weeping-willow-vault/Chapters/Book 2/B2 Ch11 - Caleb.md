# Book 2 · Chapter 11 — MIDPOINT: Caleb
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Day 45** · **3,073 words** · **LOCKED**
**THE MIDPOINT**
**Location:** The van, the drive inland, then [[Caleb Ward]]'s apartment

---

## Goal
**[[Willow]] returns to a previous subject because the partial result was unsatisfactory, and finishes him. Caleb Ward dies.**

## What happens
**He calls Iris.** He does not call people. He calls because **a fact requires reporting**, and he reports it in the flat correct register he has used since Book 1 Ch13:

> ***There's someone here.***

**No alarm, no request, no urgency, because nothing in him objects to anything.**

**She arrives after.** The Ch6 mechanism: the body keeps working for a while. He is sitting down and he answers her, **and every answer is correct and there is nothing behind any of them** — and **even the settled diminished version she had been checking on a schedule is already gone.**

**He answers her questions about his own emptying, accurately, until he stops.** At some point she stops asking, **and the reason she stops is not stated.**

He does not resist, because nothing in him objects to anything. **He is not afraid, because the equipment that produces fear was taken in Book 1 and he never got it back.**

## The form of the chapter
**Willow is barely on the page, if at all.** No confrontation, no violence.

> **The chapter is a phone call, a drive, and a man ending politely.**

## The outcome
He dies. **There is no event in it.** She is the only person present, the only person who understands what happened, **and the only person unable to feel it.**

## The face
**After Caleb — the first whole structure she has ever taken — something settles.** Iris sees her at distance and cannot say what changed. See [[Willow]].

## Why him
**The reader has already grieved him once and got him back as a diminished version they had to accept. Killing that version takes something twice.**
