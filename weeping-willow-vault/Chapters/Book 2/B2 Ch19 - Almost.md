## Goal

## The face
**She passes for a person to a stranger, for most of a minute, in ordinary daylight.**

**No character in Book 2 recognizes whose face it is.** Virek could. **Virek is not in this book.**

## Virek's ticket, as a record and not a scene
Iris surfaces his escalation ticket: **unaccounted integration events, an autonomy constraint that does not match its logged value. Raised once, six years ago.**

[[Minor Characters|Raman]]'s response is on the file **and is entirely reasonable** — the funding failed, the site is consolidating, everything in the substrate is scheduled for termination when the wind-down completes next spring. **All of that is true.**

> **The ticket is closed by the man who raised it, with no complaint. That is the whole record.**
> **A man noticed, asked once, got a good answer, and stopped.**

> [!warning] Do not make it poignant. Raman's answer stays correct.

## The outcome
**Iris has a name. Simon Virek.** She cannot find him. **No current address, no employer, no record after the shutdown. He is alive somewhere and he has not been anywhere in six years.**

## The defect class this chapter produced — the most dangerous so far
> [!warning] The competence floor
> An institutional response inadequate to the risk **as visible at the time** does not weaken a
> scene, **it inverts the book** — from *nothing in the system was built to catch this* into
> *these people were bad at their jobs.*
>
> **Walk the incident-response chain on every institutional scene:** anomaly → containment →
> operator → affected systems → immediate mitigation → monitoring → ownership → closure
> criteria → **and what later changed.**
>
> **Put the failure in a changed assumption, never in a missing step.**
