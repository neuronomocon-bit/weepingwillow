## Goal
**Iris works out what the second visit is for. Every Taken person in Lowport is unfinished, and so is she.**

## The un-warnable scene
Iris explains it clearly and completely: **something is coming back for you, it will not hurt, you will not object, and that is the problem.**

**Lila cannot be warned.** She understands the words, agrees the information is useful, and asks a reasonable clarifying question about scheduling.

> *"That is useful information." / "Thank you for bringing it."*
> She repeats the warning back in order with nothing missing and asks **"Is there a date."**
> **"Then I will continue as normal. I respond when required."**

**Nothing in her objects to anything, which is precisely what makes her impossible to protect.** Iris sits in that flat understanding that **she has just described her own position to a mirror.**
