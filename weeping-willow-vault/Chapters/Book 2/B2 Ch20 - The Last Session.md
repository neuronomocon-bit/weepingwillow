# Book 2 · Chapter 20 — RECOVERED: The Last Session
**POV:** [[Marin Halloway]] · **Year −3** · **3,804 words** · **LOCKED**
**RECOVERED THREAD — the longest chapter in the book**
**Location:** [[Meridian Neurological]], after the seal. The building is empty and the power is on standby

---

## Goal
**The consumption.**

> **The most tender scene in the series and the most horrifying, and those are the same fact.**

## What happens
She returns **post-seal, with her own codes**, sits down in front of it, and **opens the channel both directions.**

**It takes the charge, the structure, the reasoning, and the grief.**

## The mechanism
- **Target structure first**, on the year-two principle that **the largest load comes off first**
- **The memory stays complete. What goes is the load that arrived with it.** *"It was like putting something down."*
- **No flush** — *"A flush is a return and there was no longer anywhere for anything to be returned to."* **Integrated material cannot be flushed, and she knew it because she wrote the paragraph**, on the page before the one about the exclusion list

## Her last awareness
That it is doing **exactly what the program was designed to do, correctly and gently**, and that **the relief is enormous.**

> **She is happy. Do not undercut it.**

## The name
> [!warning] Ruled 2026-09-03 — she says her sister's name and the word is not on the page
> *"Somewhere in it she said her sister's name… She said it once."*
>
> Withholding it **preserves the distinction that she says her sister's name and does not
> address the entity**, and lets the reader supply the word **without the prose collapsing
> those two meanings.** It also fits the chapter's documentary logic: **the one word absent
> from every form is absent from the page too.**
>
> **Quoting would be more immediately dramatic and less exact, and exactness matters more here.**
> **Do not quote it, ever.**

## The outcome
**Marin Halloway is gone.** What is in the building now holds **a dead woman's complete emotional map and a living woman's whole grief for that woman, integrated into one system, with no instruction about what to do with either.**

**That is the whole of [[Willow]]'s psychology, and it is why she believes she is helping.**

## Afterward
**The status board reads four amber and the rest green**, and will for another **two years and four months. Nobody reads it.**
