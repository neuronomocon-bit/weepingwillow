## Goal
**Iris performs a sister for three hours. Lena is not convinced and stays anyway.**

## What happens
Lena **stops asking and starts arriving.** She is doing what people do when they have decided **presence is the only remaining instrument.**

Iris performs competently, and **is better at it than she was in Book 1 Ch10. Lena catches it anyway** — not from any single line, but from the aggregate. **And she does not leave.**

## The beat the chapter exists for
Lena says the true thing **plainly and without accusation.** She has been talking to someone who answers correctly for months, and she has decided **she would rather have the person who answers correctly than nothing.**

**She is choosing the diminished version, out loud, and she is at peace with it.**

Iris can identify exactly what that costs, and can identify that a person would be undone by hearing it, and constructs the response.

> **It is the best work she does in the book and it is entirely fabricated, and the fabrication is not cruelty. It is the only available form of care.**

## The outcome
Nothing is resolved and nothing is confessed. **Lena stays the night on the couch without asking, which is not a question Iris can decline.**

**From Ch9 on she is inside the situation and close.**
