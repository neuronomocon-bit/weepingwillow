## The outcome
**The curve has an origin point, and it is inside [[Meridian Neurological]]**, and it runs continuously **from a Tuesday three and a half years ago to a body on the north side two days ago.**

## Pell — the second half of the turn
The record Iris surfaces: **[[Minor Characters|Pell]]**, substrate technician, night rotation. Goes in for a routine check, makes contact, **comes out functional, correct and pleasant, and finishes his shift.**

**The first Taken person in the world**, and he never knows.

What reaches the reader is his supervisor's note, dated two days later, observing that **he had stopped being difficult about the things he was always difficult about.** It is not a complaint — **it reads as mild approval, and it was written by a man filling in a shift report.**

> **The first Taken person in the world is a line in a performance file, and it is the nicest thing anyone ever wrote about him.**

> [!warning] Three and a half years, not six
> Pell cannot precede the deviation, and he precedes the shutdown and causes it to move.
> The gap to Rourke's earliest case is **about eighteen months, not three years.** See [[Master Timeline]].
