# Book 2 · Chapter 6 — Too Much
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Day 37** · **3,276 words** · **LOCKED**
**Location:** A residential block on the north side. A stairwell, a flat, an ambulance bay

---

## Goal
**The first death that is unambiguously a killing. The dose has increased.**

## What happens
A man on the north side who was fine yesterday, found sitting down. **He answered the first responders correctly and stopped answering some hours later. The stopping has no event in it.**

**Everything removed at once, on a first visit.** He is not in [[Elena Rourke]]'s caseload and was never partially Taken.

> [!warning] He has a name and the reader never gets it
> The neighbor gives it to Iris, she writes it down, and she uses it once — to send it to
> Rourke and get back one word. **Do not put it on the page and do not give him a scene.**
> **Iris arrives in time to be told what he said, not in time to hear it.**

## The outcome
**Iris establishes that the effect has a magnitude, and that the magnitude has changed.**

## The drafting lesson this chapter produced
The first draft came in at **2,220 words with three beats missing** and finished at 3,280.

> **Count the brief's beats against the draft BEFORE the word count.** A chapter with a missing
> beat has no hole in it. **It is simply shorter and smooth, and only counting beats finds it.**
