## Goal
Establish the program as **legitimate and working**, and introduce both sisters through the act of one admitting the other.

> [!warning] Two load-bearing requirements
> **The reader must like [[Willow Halloway]] immediately, and must believe the science.**

## What happens
Willow enrolls. **[[Minor Characters|Terri Roth]] books her in and gets a real laugh out of her inside ninety seconds** — and [[Simon Virek]] hears that laugh from down the corridor, **which is why he can recognize her face six years later in Book 3.**

The baseline map is taken **across most of a day.** Twelve sessions from signing.

## The identifier is planted here
**MN-R-0338.** Willow reads it off the second page of the consent pack, in the institutional voice, **and calls it a parking space.**

> [!info] The reader knows whose number it is from this chapter. Iris does not until Ch15.
> **That gap is the engine of the book.**

## The consent conversation
> [!warning] It is not adequate informed consent, and that is intentional
> Marin gives her frightened sister **the plain version of an answer instead of the version
> with the intervals in it**, which is what any decent clinician does, **and it is also the
> thing that turns out to matter.**
> **Show the choice. Say nothing about it.** See [[Marin Halloway]].
