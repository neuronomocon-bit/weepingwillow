## Goal
**The turn, twice.** Marin finds the baseline is still resident. **Iris finds the name.**

## Marin's half
She **signs the closure form.** Deceased-patient closure purges the working set; **it does not purge the pre-treatment baseline map**, which is research data, which is kept.

**She constructs the technicality while standing there, in about nine seconds, and it is airtight, and she knows exactly what it is.**

**Eleven days later she opens it. Read-only.** Confirms the index is complete and closes it again.

[[Minor Characters|Terri Roth]] has the paperwork ready and says something ordinary and kind — **the last time anyone in that building treats Marin as a bereaved sister rather than a colleague.** Four lines, and it is not signaled.

## Two facts it sets that are load-bearing for Act III
1. **The research-archive access log now has two lines in it — and no one reads the log.** That is the mechanism the deviation runs through
2. **The withdrawal route Marin did not take exists, is one side of A4, and she approved its wording**

## Iris's coda — the name
The index comes up and **the number resolves.**

**Three checks, all run flat:**
1. **The first join returns a man**, provably wrong on dates
2. **Then the reuse** — MN-R-0338 was issued twice
3. **Then the degraded slice**

**The record that survives intact is the dead one, because nobody touched it after it closed. Nothing about that is ever stated.**

> [!warning] Nobody explains the name. Iris finds it.
> **That is not the same thing, and the distinction is the whole rule.**
