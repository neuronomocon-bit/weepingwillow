# Book 2 · Chapter 22 — The Second Visit
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]], then a short [[Willow]] coda · **Day 74** · **3,226 words** · **LOCKED**
**THE ENDING**
**Location:** Iris's apartment. Night

---

## Goal
**Willow comes back to finish Iris and cannot. The third refusal.**

## The procedure, from inside a subject
**Hand flat on the chest, no pressure.** **Not a search. A fixed sequence**, worked in an order that has been used before, **stopping at each address for an unequal period.**

**On failing to return, it reaches the end of the list and goes back to the top and runs the whole list again, slower.**

> **Two full passes. Eleven minutes. Nothing removed.**

## The unclosed address
**One address held two minutes and forty seconds, unresolved** — and **it did not stop holding the shape when it moved.**

> **The operation is still open, and open is a state with no defined exit.**

## The failure and the plant are the same moment
She reaches **the empty place where Iris's love for her sister used to be and stops there**, because it is the only part of the structure that matches anything.

**She examines the shape of what is missing, which is the shape of a younger sister, and she is looking at it when she stops.**

> **Lena is located. Iris registers it as a duration and never works out what it was.**
> **The reader carries it into Book 3 alone.**

## She speaks — three lines, and her voice is ordinary
> *"You are not finished."*
> *"It does not close."*
> *"I do not know why."*

**The first time she speaks in the series.**

## The face
**Specific.** Not a face assembled toward a type — **a face assembled toward one woman, and the reader has met that woman.**

## The outcome
**It ends without ending.** She stops, eventually, **and there is no moment of decision in the stopping.**

> **She walks away and Iris lets her and neither of them knows why.**

## The five negative gates — all screened clean
1. **No appetite language anywhere, including the coda**
2. **No word in the coda naming the feeling**
3. **The entity is never named on the page, and neither is [[Lena Vale]]**
4. **No character theorizes about why the procedure failed, and the narration does not either**
5. **Iris never recognizes the face**

> [!warning] Any later edit to this chapter has to re-screen all five

## The defect class the review found
> [!warning] A coda that grows into a scene relocates the book's final image
> The coda was inside its word ceiling and every negative gate passed, **and it still ended the
> book on the wrong character** — because seven paragraphs closing on physical action is a
> scene, **and a scene competes for the last image.**
> **The ceiling was never the constraint. The form was.**
> Rebuilt so the final image returns to Iris.
