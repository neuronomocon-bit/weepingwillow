# Book 1 · Chapter 4 — Lila Mercer
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act I — Discovery**
**Location:** [[Lila Mercer]]'s flat, second floor

---

## What happens
The first fully Taken person Iris interacts with directly. Lila is **calm, present, functional.** She makes eye contact, answers questions, goes through the motions.

**There is nothing behind it. The surface is intact. The interior is gone.**

## The physical response
Iris feels the wrongness **viscerally rather than emotionally** — a pressure, a recognition. **Her arms and shoulders tighten in that chair, and she sits in the van afterward until it lets go.**

> [!warning] Book 2 Ch14 inverts this exactly, and never names it
> She runs the same check in the same flat, waits, and **nothing comes up.**
> **That is the measure of her erosion, and no line comments on it.**

## Function
**This is what "Taken" looks like: no drama, just absence.** It establishes the outcome before the reader ever sees it happen.

Revision note: Lila's question mark was corrected to a period in the deep audit — **Taken characters use periods.** See [[The Taking]].
