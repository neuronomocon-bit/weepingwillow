# Book 1 · Chapter 3 — The First Case
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act I — Discovery**
**Location:** Joel and Leah's home, Lowport north side

---

## What happens
[[Daniel Kade]] flags a behavioral anomaly case: **Joel**, case ref **K-01**. A dock worker who was gone for two days and came back *"fine."* Functional but hollow — responses correct and empty.

His register: **"I know what is necessary." · "It was appropriate."**

**Leah**, his wife, shows the cost from the other side: confused, frightened, unable to explain what changed. **She mirrors what [[Lena Vale]] experiences with Iris, at a smaller scale.**

## The outcome
Iris's report to Kade: **"Something has been removed."**

Something about it feels familiar and she cannot place why. **The first unconscious connection between what happened to her and what is happening to others.**
