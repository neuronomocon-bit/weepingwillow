# Book 1 · Chapter 11 — Caleb Shift
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]], observing · **Act II — Escalation**
**Location:** An active job site, outer zone

---

## What happens
Kade sent coordinates with [[Caleb Ward]]'s name attached. Caleb is near a temporary rig, speaking to workers — **posture loose, balanced, gesturing.** A grin when he sees Iris.

**Still recognizably Caleb.** *"You want the official version or the useful one."*

**But the micro-delays are everywhere:** a pause a fraction longer than expected, a smile that stays a moment too long then drops, an answer that lands slightly late. **A joke about an old unit — the timing was off, the line landed, the reaction did not, and he did not adjust.**

## What he says about the site
> *"Nothing's broken. Everything's just… off."*
> *"Timing. Stuff lags. Not enough to fail. Enough to notice."*
> *"Like the system's thinking about it before it does anything."*
> *"You ever get the sense something's already happened and we're just catching up to it?"*

## Iris's summary
> **"Function intact. Continuity intact. Something else — slightly displaced."**

## The closing beat
> **"He had been the most alive person she knew. The tense registered. Past. She corrected it. He is. The correction held. Not fully."**

The chapter ends without confirmation. *"Nothing had happened. Nothing confirmed. The pattern continued."*
