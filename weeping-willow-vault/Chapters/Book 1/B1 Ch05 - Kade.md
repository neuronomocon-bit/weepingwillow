**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act I — Discovery**

## What happens
[[Daniel Kade]] enters more fully. He hired Iris for the Meridian job and now has more work: adjacent sites, related infrastructure. **He knows something went wrong. He does not know what.**

The relationship is professional and uneasy. **He needs her. She needs answers.**

## What it establishes
- **Meridian's scope** — multiple sites: primary, secondary storage, offsite data mirrors. **Six confirmed adjacent locations** in Iris's folder
- **Risk management: the sites were spread deliberately.** Asked who from, the answer is **"From themselves"**
- **Iris was inside Meridian for three hours**, with a **34-minute gap** inside that
- She works **"more efficiently"** now. Kade notes she would have **"pushed harder"** before
- The terms: **"Access, retrieval, no questions that get written down"**
- Her closing assessment: **"It's moving"**

> [!info] "From themselves" is a Book 2 obligation
> Book 2 Ch3 pays off the deliberate site-spread as **evidence, not dialogue.** It is the reason
> the research archive and the clinical index were separated at all.
