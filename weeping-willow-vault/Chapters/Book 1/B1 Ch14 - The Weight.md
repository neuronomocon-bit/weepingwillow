# Book 1 · Chapter 14 — The Weight
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act II — Escalation**

---

## What happens
Iris tries to process the loss. **She can recognize it intellectually and struggles to feel the grief.** Her own condition worsens. [[Daniel Kade]] notices and says nothing directly.

## Rourke
**63 new cases. No recoveries.** She says **"No"** faster than before.

> *"You used to elaborate." / "The data doesn't require elaboration."*

**Her steadiness has tightened, not cracked.**

## The measurements
- **Retrieval lag:** a site address delayed three to four seconds. *"Duration: longer than prior instances. Category: retrieval lag."*
- **Performing grief:** Kade asks *"Does it bother you"* — Iris says **"Yes"** with the correct tone and no internal state. **"She had not been describing a condition. She had been answering."**

## The two possibilities she cannot resolve
**Either she was dishonest, or she had forgotten to continue.** She cannot determine which.
