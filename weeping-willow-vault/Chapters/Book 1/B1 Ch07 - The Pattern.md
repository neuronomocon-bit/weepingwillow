# Book 1 · Chapter 7 — The Pattern
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **ACT I TURN**

---

## What happens
Iris maps the cases geographically and chronologically. **A pattern emerges — not random, not a cluster. Concentrated, and moving.**

Her method: a **time-filtered gradient**, a **directional path** rather than a cluster, with an infrastructure overlay filtered to Meridian-adjacent systems.

The transcripts share a structure: **"Question → Answer → Stop."** — **"Different words. Same shape."**

## The proximity sense, on the page
> **"Location: frontal. Duration: brief. Intensity: minimal."**

This is where the ability is established. **Book 2 adds technique, not power.**

## The turn, spoken aloud
> *"It's not a condition."*
> *"It's not environmental."*
> *"Something is moving."*
> *"And it touches people."*

She presents it to [[Daniel Kade]]. **He does not want to believe it.**
