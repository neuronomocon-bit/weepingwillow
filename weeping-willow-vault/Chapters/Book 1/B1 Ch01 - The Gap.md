# Book 1 · Chapter 1 — The Gap
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act I — Discovery**
**Location:** [[Meridian Neurological]], sealed site on the [[Lowport]] coast

---

## What happens
Iris enters the sealed facility on a routine data recovery contract, job ref **MN-44-7**, client redacted. In through a **cut fence** and a **side service door with a dead keypad**, into a concrete bunker on the coast with partial power and every third light working in the server room.

The facility is wrong in ways the prose never labels: standby power rising and falling, relays **"not synchronized, not random either,"** a wall calendar stopped three years earlier, thin dust with no drifted paper.

**Contact happens in fragments.** Memories surface — *kitchen at 19, yellow mug, hospital corridor, red scarf on a winter beach, two girls on a staircase.* A pale hand. A flash of a face: **"Features learned from observation rather than born to them."**

She comes to kneeling by her pack with no memory of the gap. **14:07 to 14:41.**

## The hook
Driving back, she finds a photo of [[Lena Vale]] under a water bottle in the cup holder — printed, softened corners. **She recognizes Lena. She feels nothing.** She leaves the photo on the dashboard.
