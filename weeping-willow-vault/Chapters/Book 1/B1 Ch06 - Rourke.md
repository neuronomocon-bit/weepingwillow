# Book 1 · Chapter 6 — Rourke
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act I — Discovery**

---

## What happens
Dr. [[Elena Rourke]] appears, tracking the anomaly cases from the medical side. **She has data, framework, and confidence.** Her model: neurological, environmental, treatable.

She dismisses Iris's observations. **Friction established — Rourke is not wrong about the symptoms, she is wrong about the cause.**

## What it establishes
- **Hundreds of cases**, classified as **behavioral flattening, dissociative presentation, emotional attenuation**
- Neural imaging: reduced activity in specific emotional processing centers — **"not uniform, targeted"**
- **No cases recover fully. Treatment is management**
- **Rourke detects Iris presenting with the same markers**: flattened affect, delayed response, reduced emotional variance
- She gives Iris **redacted case summaries on a tablet** — pragmatic, not an alliance

> [!info] The de-identification matters later
> Because the summaries are redacted, Book 2 Ch14 leaves Iris with **four hundred and nine
> people and five names.** That is why the warning cannot be delivered.

## The emotional check
> **"Expected: frustration. Observed: none."**

The first instance of the logging formula that becomes her voice. **It must vary or advance every time it recurs.**
