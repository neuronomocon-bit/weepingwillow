# Book 1 · Chapter 10 — Lena
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act II — Escalation**
**Location:** A small café off a residential street. Lena chose the place

---

## What happens
[[Lena Vale]] has been trying to reach her sister. **Iris performs the entire scene** — not transparent about the gap, constructing appropriate responses throughout.

Iris arrived first: **table by the window, clear sightline to the entrance.**

## The performances
- **"I'm okay"** — smooth, immediate
- **"I still care"** — clean, no hesitation
- **"In the ways that matter"** — a constructed answer
- Pushed on *how* she cares: **"Showing up. Staying in contact. Maintaining—"** then stops and adjusts. **She is building the response from a list, not from feeling**
- **"I'm monitoring it"** — a clinical stand-in for concern

## What Lena brings
She has **consulted a doctor**, described Iris's behavior, and been told stress or dissociation.

> *"You sound like you're managing a problem." / "I am." / "You are the problem."*

Her list: **"You used to push back. You used to argue with me. You used to care if I was annoyed with you."**

## The closing exchange
> *"I love you." / "I love you."*
> **Words matched. Tone aligned. No delay.**
> Lena's expression: **uncertain, searching, almost convinced. Then not.**

She nods, says *"Okay,"* and leaves. **Iris does not catalog the gap.** She stands, pays, steps outside. *"The city moved around her. She joined it."*
