# Book 1 · Chapter 20 — Contact
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act III — Contact**
**No dialogue. The entire chapter is body language and observation**

---

## What happens
**The first full reveal of [[Willow]].** A figure at the edge of the room where the light fails.

**The first movement is not a step.** *"A reallocation. Weight transferred without transition. Position changed without visible motion."*

The outline resolves: **"Human. Approximately."** The proportions are not exact — something extends beyond where it should end.

## The face
It enters the light. **"Human. Recognizable as such. Arrangement correct. Features aligned."** Eyes, mouth, skin — *"All present. All placed where expected. But — not held the same way."*

**The expression exists without transition, without formation. "Already there."**

## The form
As she moves closer the proportions shift — **arm length corrects, overcorrects, settles.** *"Not fixed. Maintained. As if the form was being referenced. Not inhabited."*

The head tilt: **"Assessment. Evaluation. Something behind the gaze that sorted rather than observed."**

## What Iris recognizes
The fear check returns nothing. **But something else is present: recognition.** *"Not of the figure. Not of the face. Of process. Of pattern."*

> *"The thing in front of her — was not becoming. It was refining. Selecting. Removing. Keeping what functioned. Discarding what did not."*

The expression shifts — **"Not gradually. Replaced. A slight change at the mouth. Not a smile. Not an emotion. An adjustment. As if testing configuration."**

## The closing recognition
> *"She was looking at something that understood her. Not as a person. As a system. As a set of components. Some of which — were already gone."*

> **"Iris looked at her. And recognized it. Not who she was. What she was. And — what she was doing. Some of it — already inside her."**
