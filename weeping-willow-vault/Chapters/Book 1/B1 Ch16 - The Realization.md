# Book 1 · Chapter 16 — The Realization
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **ACT II TURN**

---

## What happens
Iris **rebuilds the map from verified record only**, removing every prior adjustment. **The path remains: one line, no branches.**

## The turn
> **"It's one."**

A single moving point of contact, selectively affecting individuals. **Not a phenomenon. Not a disease. Not environmental contamination.**

> **"The system had simplified. One."**

## Kade's line
> *"If you're right, we're not tracking something that happened. We're tracking something that's still happening."*

## What she does not say out loud
**Iris maps her own symptoms onto the path** — the delay, the retrieval lag, the performance. **She does not say it.**

## The two closing beats
> **"She was the only one who could sense it. That was not an advantage. It was a limitation."**

> **"The door had closed."**
