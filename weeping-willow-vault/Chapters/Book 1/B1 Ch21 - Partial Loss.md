# Book 1 · Chapter 21 — Partial Loss
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act III — Contact**
**No dialogue. Continuous with Ch20**

---

## What happens
[[Willow]] moves first — a single step, measured, exact. The field holds Iris in place. **"Not restrained. Not forced. Held."**

Willow lifts her hand, smooth and continuous, **"As if the endpoint had already been determined."** Iris tries to move: the signal forms and does not complete.

## The contact
> **"Light. Minimal. Sufficient. No force. No impact. Just — connection."**

**The effect is immediate.** Something inside softens. *"Dissolved. Edges blurred. Definition lost."*

Iris tracks the change in real time — **not a location but a function.** *"A response. A reflex. Something automatic. Something she used — without thinking."* It falters. **"The pathway remained. The result did not."**

## What is left
She searches and finds the space, **clear and defined.** *"The shape of it — intact. But the content — absent."* She reaches for it. *"No trace. No residual. Just — absence."*

She forces movement and this time it completes. **Contact breaks immediately. Willow does not follow and does not reach again.**

## The inventory
> *"Functions present. Responses available. One — missing."*

Defined, specific, unrecoverable. She tries to name it: **"No reference. No label. Only — gap."**

Testing it is **"Like pressing against something that should resist — and finding no surface."**

**The system auto-corrects.** *"She adjusted. Without deciding to. The system corrected. Continued."*

## The closing beat
> *"Feeling the absence. Not what had been lost. Just — where it had been."*
