## What happens
Iris and [[Daniel Kade]] investigate more cases together. Each reinforces the pattern: **contact-based, localized, progressive.**

## What it establishes
- **Kade's Meridian access codes still work.** Clearance was never revoked
- **Site types:** **relay point** (data movement) and **transfer point** (equipment). He recognized both from his logistics work
- **His guilt, stated plainly and only once:** *"I moved things through here. Crates. Equipment. Data units. Didn't ask what was in them." "Didn't ask why."*
- **Proximity sense at the third site:** a faint shift that does not resolve, and fades. **"Something" / "Nothing"**

## The erosion beat
Iris watches Kade's pauses and **notes them structurally rather than emotionally.** She recognizes the gap. **She does not feel the need to close it.**
