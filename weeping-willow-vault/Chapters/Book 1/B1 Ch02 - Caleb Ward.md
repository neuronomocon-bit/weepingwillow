# Book 1 · Chapter 2 — Caleb Ward
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act I — Discovery**
**Location:** Another job, another site

---

## What happens
[[Caleb Ward]] is introduced through a work interaction. Sharp, conversational, alive — **the baseline for "normal human" before the horror strips it away.**

He notices something off about Iris: slower, flatter, less reactive than he remembers. **Iris deflects. She does not know how to explain it.**

## His line
> *"If something's off, you can just say that. You don't have to—"* he gestured vaguely *"—optimize the answer."*

> [!warning] The locked fragment is "optimize the answer"
> Broken by a gesture and two em dashes. **It has never existed as one contiguous sentence.**
> A one-line quotation of it was wrong for months and was corrected 2026-08-31. See [[Audit Method]].

## Function
He is one of the few people who knew Iris before the incident, which is what makes him able to see the change. **The reader connects to him here so that Book 1 Ch12 and Book 2 Ch11 both land.**
