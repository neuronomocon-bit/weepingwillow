# Book 1 · Chapter 15 — Deterioration
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act II — Escalation**

---

## What happens
Her symptoms intensify. **The horror of the chapter is that the person trying to stop the threat is becoming unreliable — and she knows it.**

## The false certainties
- **A phantom case.** She is certain she marked it. There is no record
- **A false site memory.** The interior does not match the exterior
- **An unrecalled conversation with Kade**
- **A burned contact** flagged her to Kade: *"said you came in wrong."* The messages are the same but **the order has shifted, and something is missing between them**

## The self-note
> **"Do not trust sequence."**

She reopens it later and **understands the words but not the conclusion. She continues without verification.**

## The map corruption
She adjusts a marker to fit the path. **The coordinates do not match. She leaves it.**

## The closing formula
> **"Certain. Incorrect. Continuing."**
