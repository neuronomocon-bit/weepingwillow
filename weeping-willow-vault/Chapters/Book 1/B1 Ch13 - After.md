# Book 1 · Chapter 13 — After
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act II — Escalation**

---

## What happens
Iris sees [[Caleb Ward]] after. **He recognizes her. He talks. He functions.** The ease, the sharpness, the conversational rhythm are flattened.

**His answers are clean, immediate, correct — no excess, no deviation, just acceptance.**

## The two tests
- **The joke test.** *"I don't recall"* — and when prompted, **"That's consistent."** Not the same as the original, **filled with something sufficient**
- **The key test.** *"If everything went wrong at once"* — the old Caleb would improvise. **This Caleb gives textbook crisis management**

## The closing line
> **"The difference between them was not direction. Only distance."**

## The mirror
**Caleb is now what [[Lila Mercer]] was in Ch4 — but this time the reader knew him before.** Iris is looking at what she might become.
