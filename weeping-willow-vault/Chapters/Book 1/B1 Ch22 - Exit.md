# Book 1 · Chapter 22 — Exit
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act III — Contact · THE ENDING**
**No dialogue. Day 0 of [[Book 2 - The Ending]] is the end of this chapter**

---

## What happens
Iris steps back and [[Willow]] does not follow. *"The exchange was over. Iris understood that. Not from signal. Not from movement. From absence."*

**Willow lets her go. The question hangs and is never answered: why?**

She retraces the Ch18 path exactly — the corridor, the water along the wall still and undisturbed, the first warehouse, **the door still open, "Not forced. Left that way."**

*"Nothing marked her passage. Nothing marked Willow's. Only — absence."*

The field recedes incrementally until the pressure returns to what it was before: **"Persistent. Manageable. Incomplete."**

## Back in Lowport
The familiar café, interior unchanged. The server asks a question; **Iris responds correctly; the exchange completes.**

**[[Lena Vale]]'s message arrives.** *"Text. Direct. Personal. Content that would have produced a response. Previously."* She reads it once, understands it, closes it. **No reply.**

> [!info] Book 2 Ch1 opens on that thread, stacked back through weeks to the night she did not answer the first

## What she has confirmed
> *"Willow existed. Singular. Active. Selecting. Refining. And — aware. Of her."*

## The dual comparison — the book's closing idea
> *"She was closer. To Willow. Than anyone else alive. That was true. She was also — further from herself. That was also true."*

**She does not know which of those should frighten her.** She evaluates both: no prioritization, no weighting, no response.

## The final fear check
> *"She noted that she should be afraid. The condition was appropriate. Expected. Aligned with context. She waited. No change. The fear did not form."*

> **"She sat within it. Present. Operating. Reduced. She waited. Nothing came."**
