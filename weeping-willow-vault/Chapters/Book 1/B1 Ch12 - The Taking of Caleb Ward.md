# Book 1 · Chapter 12 — The Taking of Caleb Ward
**POV: [[Caleb Ward]] — the only chapter from his perspective in the book**
**MIDPOINT · Act II — Escalation**

---

## What happens
**The first on-page Taking.** No spectacle, no chase. It is quiet and intimate.

[[Willow]] touches him. He goes still. Then it is over. He stands, looks at his surroundings, and functions.

**Willow is silent throughout. Hand on chest, flat, no pressure. Her face is not retained.**

## The memories that surface
- **Running as a kid**
- **Coffee on a late shift** — *the warmth of it, the fact that they had noticed*
- **A job gone wrong**, and the improvised solution

## The mechanism, stated once
> **Feeling separates from image, then image follows. "Gone."**

**The final thing taken is not an event but a state — the sense that things mattered.**

## After
He logs: *"Response timing remains inconsistent. No confirmed failure."* — **processing the event through the reduced framework.**

> **"Nothing in him marked the change. Nothing in him noted the absence."**

## Why this is the scene that defines the book's horror
**Not violence. Subtraction.**

> The second cup already down off the shelf, and he leaves it where it is.
> **Do not cut it and do not explain it.**
