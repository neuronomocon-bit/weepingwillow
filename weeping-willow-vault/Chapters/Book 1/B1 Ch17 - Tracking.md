# Book 1 · Chapter 17 — Tracking
**POV:** [[Iris Vale]] · **Act III — Contact**
**Location:** [[Lowport]]'s outer zones, moving toward the coast and industrial perimeter

---

## What happens
Opens past the last mapped point — **"Everything ahead was inferred."** Iris follows directional pull rather than data.

[[Daniel Kade]]'s warning is acknowledged and not followed: **"Constraint. She was the only one who could track it. That was sufficient."**

## The call
> *"Where are you" / "Outer zone" / "Be specific."*
> *"You're not equipped to do this alone" / "Yes"* — **agreement, not compliance**
> *"You won't be able to track it" / "That's not the point" / "It is."*
> *"Send your position" / "I am" / "Stay where you are" / "I won't."*

## The central realization
> **"She was not following a signal. Not exactly. She was following change in herself. She was measuring proximity through loss."**

## The fear check
Expected response appropriate to context: **none.** No heart rate increase, no hesitation, no aversion. **She noted the absence.**

## The ending
Pressure stabilizes at maximum sustained level. She steps forward and there is no further increase. **"She had reached it. Or — it had reached her."**

She does not call Kade, does not mark the location, does not step back.

> **"The pressure held. The absence did. She had followed it. She was here. She waited."**
