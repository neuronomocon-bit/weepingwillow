Chapter 15 — Deterioration
Iris adjusted the map.
Points marked. Movement established. Direction consistent.
She reviewed the sequence.
Entry points. Progression. Gaps.
One of the gaps had closed.
She had marked it earlier.
She was certain of that.
She searched for the entry.
It wasn't there.
She checked the timestamp filters. Re-ran the query.
Same result. No additional case.
The gap remained.
Iris paused.
She could see it. Not visually. Positionally. Where the case should be. Where it had been.
She accessed the archive. Pulled prior versions.
No record.
She held the contradiction.
Two possibilities.
The case had been removed.
Or—
it had not existed.
She selected the first.
Marked the gap as filled.
Continued.

***

The next site was inactive. No access response. No internal power. No indication of recent use.
Iris stood at the entrance.
She had been here before. Recently. She was certain of that.
She checked her device. No prior visit logged.
She held the memory. Clear. Sequence intact. Arrival. Entry. Interior layout.
She walked the perimeter, mapping it against the interior she remembered.
The walls were wrong. The spacing incorrect.
She stopped. Recalibrated.
The memory did not adjust.
She marked the site as previously visited. Logged observations based on recall.
Moved on.

***

Kade called.
"You went to the south corridor site," he said.
"Yes."
"Anything."
"No."
"Nothing."
"No."
Pause.
"That's not what you said earlier," he said.
Iris reviewed the statement.
Earlier.
She accessed the conversation. It wasn't there.
"I didn't," she said.
"You did."
"No."
Kade held the line.
"Iris."
She checked her outgoing messages. No record.
"I didn't," she repeated.
Another pause. Longer.
"Alright," Kade said. "Send me what you have."
"I will."
The call ended.
Iris stood where she was. She replayed the exchange.
That's not what you said earlier.
She searched for it. No record.
She held the certainty. She had not.
She moved on.

***

At the next location, the contact didn't answer.
Iris waited. Three minutes. Then five.
She checked the message thread. Her last message was direct.
I'm here.
No response.
She reviewed the prior exchange. The tone had been neutral. Informational. No escalation.
She waited another two minutes. Then left.
Kade called again.
"You lost the contact," he said.
"Yes."
"You pushed too hard."
"No."
"Iris."
She pulled up the thread again. Read it line by line.
"No," she said again.
"He flagged you," Kade said. "Said you came in wrong."
Iris held the phone.
"That's not accurate," she said.
Silence.
"I'm looking at it," Kade said. "It is."
Iris re-read the exchange.
The words remained the same.
But something shifted.
Not the content. The order.
The second message. It had not been second. It had come later. After—
something else.
Missing.
She scrolled. Nothing between.
She checked timestamps. Aligned. No gap.
She sat with it. Both versions present. Neither giving way.
"He's mistaken," she said.
Kade didn't respond immediately.
"We'll talk later," he said.
The line closed.
Iris remained where she was.
The messages did not change. Her memory did not adjust.
Both held.
They did not align.
She closed the file.

***

Back at her apartment, she opened a new document.
She wrote:
Do not trust sequence.
She paused. Then added:
Verify against external record before acting.
She read it once. Saved the file. Closed it.
Later, she opened it again.
The text was there. Familiar in form. Not in reasoning.
She read the first line.
Do not trust sequence.
She understood the words. Not the conclusion. Why sequence. What had failed.
No reference. No context.
She read the second line.
Verify against external record before acting.
She held it. Waited.
No connection formed.
The instruction remained. Detached.
She left the document open.
The map remained active. She adjusted one of the markers. Shifted it slightly. Aligned it with the path.
It fit. Better than before.
She checked the underlying data. The coordinates did not match.
She left it.
She marked the next site. Prepared to move. Paused.
Checked the note again.
Do not trust sequence.
She read it. Considered.
Then continued without verification.
The system held.
Her understanding of it did not.
Iris moved through the city.
Working. Adjusting.
Certain.
Incorrect.
Continuing.
