## Text Placement Guide

### Front Cover
- **Series name:** "WEEPING WILLOW" — top area, within safe zone
- **Subtitle:** "The Absence" / "The Ending" / "The Silence" — below series name
- **Author name:** Bottom area, within safe zone

### Spine (top to bottom, rotated)
- **Series + subtitle:** "WEEPING WILLOW: THE ABSENCE"
- **Author name:** near bottom
- KDP convention: spine text reads top-to-bottom when book is face-up

### Back Cover
- **Blurb/synopsis:** Upper half, within safe zone
- **Series identifier:** Optional — "Book One of Weeping Willow" etc.
- **Barcode area:** Bottom-right, 2" x 1.2" minimum, needs readable background (white or very light rectangle)

---

## Typography Suggestions

Given the dark gothic aesthetic:

- **Series title:** A clean, sharp serif or a restrained gothic face. Not ornate — precise. Something that reads as clinical against the horror. Think Trajan, Cormorant Garant, or a high-contrast modern serif.
- **Subtitle:** Lighter weight of the same family, or a contrasting sans-serif.
- **Author name:** Same family as series title, smaller, high contrast against background.
- **Color:** White or very pale grey for maximum contrast against the near-black backgrounds. Avoid gold/metallic — this isn't fantasy. Keep it cold.
- **Spine text:** Must be legible at small size. Simpler face if needed.

### Book 1 — The Absence

> The job was routine. Enter the sealed Meridian facility. Recover the data. Get out.
>
> Iris Vale got out. But something came with her — not in her equipment, not in the files. In the space where her ability to love her sister used to be.
>
> Now people across Lowport are coming back different. Calm. Functional. Hollow. The medical establishment calls it neurological. Environmental. Treatable.
>
> Iris knows better. She's felt it. Whatever is inside that facility didn't stay inside.
>
> And it's still hungry.

### Book 2 — The Ending

*(Sequel. Rewritten 2026-08-27; the prequel-era copy is dead. "The Keeping" was retired with the prequel arc.)*

> The people who came back different are starting to die.
>
> Not killed, exactly. They stop eating. They walk into traffic. They do not seek treatment, because nothing in them objects to anything anymore.
>
> Iris Vale came out of Meridian Neurological with something removed and a pattern she could not finish reading. Now Daniel Kade has opened the crates he moved the week the building went dark, and Iris does the only thing she has ever been good at. She recovers the data.

>

### Book 3 — The Silence

> It's no longer hiding. It's choosing.
>
> The entity Iris Vale has been tracking is selecting targets by the depth of their emotional lives, traveling across Lowport to find the people who feel the most. Then it reaches for her sister.

> Then the numbers get too large to ignore, and the city gets it wrong, and then the city stops minding. Crime is down. Conflict is down. Everything is improving. Some people are grateful.
>
> That's the horror.
